Loki Clone

Trickery of Loki Laufeyson

Blaster

At a whispered word and a flick of green light, a second Loki steps out of the empty air — the same sly smile, the same cutting voice, the same easy contempt, wearing the God of Mischief's face down to the last detail. It is a lie given just enough substance to matter: a conjured double that moves and strikes and taunts exactly as its maker would, sowing the one weapon Loki prizes above all others — doubt. Which of the two is the true god and which the phantom? By the time an enemy commits to an answer, the trap has usually already sprung, for the clone's purpose was never simply to fight but to make every choice on the battlefield a guess. And when at last it is struck down or its work is done, the duplicate does not fall so much as unravel, dissolving into a ribbon of smoke and the faint, fading sound of laughter — leaving its foes to wonder, uneasily, whether they ever managed to lay a hand on the real Loki at all.

"I am everywhere!"
