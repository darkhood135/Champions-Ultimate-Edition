# Champions Ultimate Edition — Release Roadmap

*Cumulative season-by-season plan for the in-fiction release timeline. Started 2026-10-29.*
*Companion to `version-history-launch-plan.md`, which covers the launch itself and the patch-notes system.*

Each Season gets an entry here once it has a plan. Seasons with no plan yet stay in the calendar as open slots. Anything wanted but not yet scheduled goes in the Backlog at the bottom.

---

## Release caps

| Season type | Champions | Battlefields |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard season** | up to **5** | **1–2** |
| **Celebration season** | up to **10** | up to **3** |

- **Celebration seasons:** every 12th season after launch, i.e. the anniversaries (Season 13, Season 25, …).
- *Open question:* do Act expansions (e.g. v2.0.0, Act II) also count as celebrations?
- Launch (Season 1) is its own scope (42 Champions, 7 battlefields) and isn't bound by these caps.

## Season 7: Icons of Horror (v1.6.0, Oct 28, 2027)

**Theme:** Halloween. The horror icons arrive through a limited-time Halloween **Event Story**.

**New franchises:** Halloween, Friday the 13th, A Nightmare on Elm Street, Scream, The Texas Chain Saw Massacre.

### Champions (5 of 5)

| Champion | Real name | Role | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Shape | Michael Myers | Striker | Locked |
| Jason | Jason Voorhees | Defender | Locked |
| Freddy | Freddy Krueger | Breaker | Locked |
| Ghostface | Billy Loomis | Plunderer | Proposed |
| Leatherface | Bubba Sawyer | Berserker | Proposed |

- **Ghostface design note:** built as a duo with **Stu Macher**, a summon that spawns at the start of battle. Weak apart, strong together. Stu steals enemy buffs and pulls enemies in; Billy goes in for the execution.
  - The Stu sheet must say explicitly what happens to Stu if Billy is defeated, and vice versa. There's no roster-wide rule for summon persistence.

### Battlefields (2 of 2)
- **Lampkin Lane** (Haddonfield): Halloween.
- **Camp Crystal Lake:** Friday the 13th.
- Each gets 1–2 signature items, per the map-centered items rule.

### Also planned this Season
- **Halloween Event Story:** the in-fiction reason these characters enter the Cataclysm. Its Story Notes ship in v1.6.0.
- **Codex:** universe entries for each new franchise, plus character entries for the new Champions.
- **Links:**
  - one Universe link per franchise;
  - a shared Link across all five (e.g. a "Slasher" Mindset link) with a Leader among them, so the horror team can actually be led under the Leader/Link economy.
- **Halloween costumes for existing Champions** (optional): a live-game staple, and it gives the Season something for players who don't use the new characters.

### Deferred from this Season
See the Backlog: Laurie Strode, Ash Williams, Billy the Puppet, Sawyer Residence, Badham Preschool.
