Goku (Super Saiyan)

Son Goku · Kakarot

Defender

Pushed past the edge of grief and rage by the murder of his closest friend, Goku ignites into the first Super Saiyan in a thousand years — a golden-haired warrior of legend risen to stand against the tyrant Frieza.

By the time he reached the dying world of Namek, Goku had already grown far beyond the warrior who once barely survived a Saiyan prince — but nothing had prepared him for Frieza. The galactic tyrant who annihilated the entire Saiyan race pushed him past every limit he had, through the Kaio-ken and a Spirit Bomb drawn from a whole world's life, and still it was not enough. Then, with Vegeta already dead at Frieza's hand and his final plea ringing in Goku's ears, the monster murdered Krillin before his eyes — and something in the gentlest heart in the universe simply broke. What rose in its place was the stuff of legend: the Super Saiyan, a transformation no living Saiyan had achieved in a thousand years, awakened not through ambition or lust for power as Vegeta had chased it, but through pure, righteous fury and the unbearable weight of loss. That is what makes this Goku so remarkable, and so terrifying to his enemies. Even wearing the power of a legend, wreathed in golden fire, he never stops being himself — the rage sharpens him but does not consume him, and when the battle is finally won, he offers even Frieza the mercy the tyrant has never once shown another living soul. The most devastating force the universe had seen in a millennium, and it was summoned by love rather than hatred.

The transformation remakes him from the ground up. His hair blazes gold, his eyes burn a piercing teal, and a corona of golden light erupts around him as every ounce of his already staggering strength, speed, and durability is multiplied many times over in an instant — turning a warrior Frieza had toyed with into the tyrant's undeniable better. All that came before remains his to command: the Kamehameha wave now loosed with world-shaking force, the ki mastery, the flight, the hard-won techniques of a lifetime and one death. But the golden power, for all its glory, was never the true measure of him. Frieza sought immortality and Vegeta craved the legend, and neither could grasp the simple truth that unlocked it: Goku became the Super Saiyan not to conquer, not to rule, and not even to win — but to avenge a fallen friend, and to protect everyone still standing behind him. And having touched the power of legend, he never once let it change who he was.

"You already know, don't you? I am a Saiyan who came from Earth in order to defeat you."

Changelog

Balance changes since this Champion's release. Newest first.

v1.0.1 September 6, 2026
  • Nerf Golden Crash (Enraged) — now hits 3 times instead of 4 (600% ATK total, down from 800%).
  • Nerf Transformation stat boosts reduced: ATK +110 → +60 and MAG +80 → +60 (Enraged ATK/MAG now 350 / 340).
  • Rework Righteous Fury of the Fabled Super Saiyan — the turn-8 automatic transformation trigger has been removed. Goku now transforms into Enraged Super Saiyan only upon first going below 20% HP, alongside the passive's existing 20% HP restore (a single "second wind" moment).
  • Nerf Final Kamehameha (Enraged ULTIMATUM) — self-applied "Taunt" shortened from 2 turns to 1, so the counter-beam is no longer near-self-guaranteed.
  • Rework Gut Punch / Immeasurable Impact — the "piercing damage" wording renamed to "Precise" to resolve a naming collision with the line-penetration term "Piercing." No mechanical change.

Launch tuning to keep the Enraged form a Defender power-spike rather than a role change. The base form was already fair; the transformation was lifting offense, sustain, and protection all at once, for free, on a guaranteed turn-8 timer. Trimming Golden Crash and the ATK/MAG boosts pulls his Enraged damage below Thor and Wonder Woman's ATK, and gating the transformation behind dropping below 20% HP turns the power spike into an earned, risk-based "second wind" — dramatic, thematic, and with real counterplay (burst him before he ignites, or chip him and he stays the modest base Defender). The Precise rename is a pure clarity fix.
