Green Lantern

Hal Jordan

Blaster

A fearless test pilot chosen by a dying alien to wield a ring powered by sheer force of will, Hal Jordan guards the cosmos as Green Lantern — protector of Sector 2814 and among the greatest of the intergalactic Corps.

Hal Jordan was reckless long before he was chosen, and being chosen only gave that recklessness something worth spending itself on. A hotshot test pilot who lived to push machines past their limits, he was selected by the ring of the dying alien Abin Sur for a single, defining quality — not that he felt no fear, but that he could master it, overcome even the greatest terror through raw willpower. That made him the first human inducted into the Green Lantern Corps, a galaxy-spanning peacekeeping force answerable to the ancient Guardians of the Universe, and it thrust an ordinary man into a role holding the line for an entire sector of space. He is cocky, impulsive, and allergic to authority — forever chafing against the Guardians' rules and going his own way — but beneath the swagger runs a well of courage and conviction that has no bottom. He has known terrible grief and terrible falls, moments where the weight of it all nearly broke him, and every time he has clawed his way back to the light, because the one thing Hal Jordan will not do is stay down. His willpower is not merely a tool he wields; it is the whole measure of the man.

That will is made manifest by the most versatile weapon in the universe. His power ring channels the green light of pure willpower into solid form, conjuring anything he can imagine — blades, shields, fists, jet engines, entire fortresses of hard green light — bounded by nothing but the strength of his resolve and the limits of his imagination. It carries him unaided across the void between stars, throws up barriers that turn aside armies, and looses blasts capable of felling the mightiest foes, so long as its lantern keeps it charged. But the ring, for all its wonders, is only ever as strong as the will that drives it — and few wills anywhere in the cosmos burn as fiercely, or as stubbornly, as Hal Jordan's. Hand that ring to a lesser man and it flickers; in Hal's grip, it blazes.

"The farther I get from the cockpit, the more I realize there's only ever two choices: Go down in flames—or do something crazy."

Flyboys w/ Iron Man

Put Hal Jordan and Tony Stark in the same room and you get two of the biggest egos in any universe, circling each other with grins and barbs. They're the same animal — hotshot aces who never met a rule they didn't want to break or a machine they couldn't push past its limits, each dead certain he's the smartest, fastest, and best thing in the sky. Hal gives as good as he gets and enjoys every second of it; the trash talk between them is half insult, half genuine delight at finding someone who can actually keep pace. Beneath the swagger, though, runs the easy trust of two men who've each stared down impossible odds and grinned — and who'd fly straight into hell for the other without hesitating, if only to prove they got there first.

Will and Discipline w/ Batman

Batman is exactly the kind of authority Hal Jordan has spent his whole life ignoring, and the feeling is thoroughly mutual. To Hal, the Dark Knight is a humorless control freak who trusts nothing he can't plan for and needs, badly, to loosen his grip. He knows full well Batman takes him for a reckless cowboy who'll get someone killed one day — and it needles him precisely because he can never quite prove the man wrong. And yet, when the chips are truly down, Hal has never once doubted that Batman will be exactly where he said he'd be — and he suspects, though he'd sooner swallow the ring than admit it, that the old man feels the very same about him. Two opposite ways of winning the same war, forever grating and forever holding.

Will Against Fear w/ Frieza

Hal Jordan took an oath to protect his sector from precisely this — a world-devouring tyrant who slaughters civilizations for profit and calls the wreckage an empire. Frieza is everything the Green Lantern Corps exists to stand against, and Hal has never in his life been more certain of a fight. He knows the emperor outclasses him on raw power, knows the ring runs on nothing but his own resolve, and throws himself in anyway — because that resolve is the one thing the tyrant's terror can't reach. Frieza rules a galaxy through fear; Hal's entire strength is the will to overcome it. If ever there were a foe his ring was forged to face, it is the emperor who has never once in his life been told no.

Changelog

Balance changes since this Champion's release. Newest first.

v1.0.1 August 26, 2026
  • Buff MAG increased from 290 to 300.

Green Lantern's kit scales almost entirely off ATK, with his MAG stat feeding only Green Gatling and the minor airborne passive. A small bump gives the ULTIMATUM a touch more weight and keeps MAG a meaningful — rather than vestigial — part of his stat line, without disturbing the rest of his balance.
