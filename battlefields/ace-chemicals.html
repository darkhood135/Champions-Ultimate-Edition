ACE Chemicals

Gotham City, New Jersey

DC Universe

The ACE Chemicals processing plant on Gotham’s industrial waterfront, closed for years and still leaking. Pipes hiss overhead, alarms nobody maintains still go off, and the Great Vat at the heart of the plant never quite drains. Raised catwalks and a rotating gantry are the only dry routes to the center, and every few turns the broken pumps flood another wing of the plant with acid. Hold the high ground and you decide who has to wade; lose it, and you’d better have found a hazmat suit first.

"I know where we are immediately. The chemical smell gives it away... Ace. Where he was born."

Stage Rules
  • Sealed Plant: The outer edge of the map is wall, not void — nothing falls off the map. The only fall on this battlefield is into the Great Vat.
  • Chemical Floods: The plant is divided into five sectors:
    West BayEast BayNorth CorridorSouth CorridorVat Hall floor
  • Alarm: At the start of battle turn 3 the alarm sounds and the chosen sector’s panels flash. The flood arrives at the start of turn 4 and lasts 2 battle turns. The cycle repeats every 3 battle turns (alarms on turns 3, 6, 9…).
  • Flooding: While a sector is flooded, every elevation-0 panel in it is Acid Spill. Raised panels and Stairs never flood.
  • Sector choice: The same sector never floods twice in a row, and the first flood can never target a spawn bay. Otherwise the sector is random, unless it was locked at the Flood Control Console.
  • No respawns: Each item spawn tile holds one item per battle. Once it is picked up, nothing respawns there.
  • Hazmat gear: Hazmat Suits only come from the two Hazmat Lockers; they never appear on item spawn tiles.
  • Environmental damage: Damage from Acid Spill, Chemical Drums and the Great Vat is environmental damage — flat, and it skips the attack calculation.

Obstacles & Terrain

Each obstacle's color matches its map panel and key swatch
Walls
Plant Wall Impassable Wall
Footprint: Varying Sizes · Blocks: Movement, Line of Sight, Projectiles · HP: Indestructible
  • Cannot be moved through, attacked through, flown over or destroyed by any means.
Storage Silo Impassable Wall
Footprint: 2x2 panels · Blocks: Movement, Line of Sight, Projectiles · HP: Indestructible
  • Cannot be moved through, attacked through or destroyed by any means.
  • The silos run to the ceiling: unlike the Water Tower, Champions with Elevation Bypass cannot path over them.
Mixing Reactor Impassable Wall
Footprint: 2x2 panels · Blocks: Movement, Line of Sight, Projectiles · HP: Indestructible
  • The plant’s central mixing machine. Cannot be moved through, attacked through or destroyed by any means.
  • Breaks line of sight across the central platform, so a Champion holding the center cannot cover both gantry decks from one panel.
Pipe Run Half Obstacle
Footprint: 2x1 panels · Blocks: Line of Sight (Partial) · HP: 400
  • Cuts the accuracy of any ranged ability shot over it in half. If the attack misses the target, it’ll hit the obstacle.
  • Champions may stand on top of this obstacle (this does not change their elevation tier).
  • When destroyed, the pipes rupture: both panels become Acid Spill for 2 battle turns.
Hazards
The Great Vat Hazard
Footprint: 48 panels (2-wide ring around the center) · Blocks: Movement · HP: Indestructible
  • Behaves as Void for movement: it cannot be walked into, and the normal Gap Jumping rules apply (crossing the 2-wide vat between a gantry deck and the central platform costs 5 STA).
  • A unit pushed, pulled or knocked into the vat is Submerged instead of falling: it takes 1,200 damage, is inflicted with "Vulnerable" for 1 turn and "Poison" for 2 turns (using a fixed ATK and MAG of 200 in place of the inflictor’s stats), then returns on the vat-edge panel it was knocked from.
  • As with Void, a unit that was not standing on a vat-edge panel catches itself on the edge instead (no damage).
  • Non-playable enemies are defeated outright when Submerged.
  • Airborne Champions cannot be Submerged; if pushed into the vat, they stop on the edge panel.
Chemical Drum Hazard · Explosive Container
Footprint: 1 panel · Blocks: Movement, Line of Sight (Partial) · HP: 300
  • Acts as a half obstacle: it cuts the accuracy of ranged abilities shot over it in half, and a missed attack hits the drum.
  • When destroyed, it explodes: every unit within 1 panel (allies and enemies) takes 800 damage, and those panels become Acid Spill for 2 battle turns.
  • Any other Chemical Drum within the blast detonates as well (chain reaction). Corroded Catwalk panels in the blast lose 1 Integrity.
  • All damage from the chain is credited to whoever destroyed the first drum.
Elevation Features
Stairs Traversable Elevation
Footprint: 1 panel · Connects: Elevation 0 and +1 · HP: Indestructible
  • Moving onto or off a Stairs panel costs the normal 1 STA per panel, with no surcharge.
  • A Champion standing on Stairs counts as being on both tiers for targeting: they can target, and be targeted by, units on either tier without elevation restrictions.
  • Stairs never flood.
Interactive Elements
Rotating Gantry Interactive
Footprint: 2x2 panels · Blocks: Nothing · HP: Indestructible
  • A +1 walkway that connects the central platform to one gantry deck at a time: West or East. Both possible positions are shown on the map.
  • Starts the battle retracted: both positions are Great Vat panels until a Gantry Control is used.
  • Moves at most once per battle turn, and never while a unit is standing on it.
Gantry Control Interactive
Footprint: 1 panel · Blocks: Movement · HP: Indestructible
  • A Champion adjacent to a Gantry Control may spend 2 STA to swing the Rotating Gantry to this side, or to retract it if it is already extended to this side.
  • One Gantry Control sits on each gantry deck.
Flood Control Console Interactive
Footprint: 2x1 panels · Blocks: Movement · HP: Indestructible
  • A Champion adjacent to the console may spend 2 STA to lock in the sector for the next Chemical Flood. It cannot lock the sector that flooded most recently.
  • The last lock made before the alarm sounds wins.
  • Locking a sector does not shorten the warning: the alarm still gives every Champion one full turn of notice.
Pipe Valve Interactive · Cooldown
Footprint: 1 panel · Blocks: Movement · HP: Indestructible
  • A Champion adjacent to a Pipe Valve may spend 2 STA to spray acid east or west along the corridor in a 3-wide, 4-long line (covering the corridor’s full width).
  • Panels in the line become Acid Spill for 2 battle turns. Units in the line take the spill’s effect immediately (this counts as their entry for the turn).
  • Each valve then has a 3-battle-turn cooldown.
Hazmat Locker Interactive · Limited
Footprint: 2x1 panels · Blocks: Movement · HP: Indestructible
  • A Champion adjacent to a locker may spend 1 STA to take a Hazmat Suit. Each locker holds 2 suits (4 per battle).
  • A Champion can only carry one Hazmat Suit at a time.
  • An empty locker becomes an inert full obstacle.
Special Tiles
Acid Spill Special Tile · Environmental Damage
Footprint: 1 panel · Blocks: Nothing · HP: Indestructible
  • A Champion who enters an Acid Spill (once per battle turn) or starts their turn on one takes 400 damage and is inflicted with "Poison" for 2 turns (using a fixed ATK and MAG of 200 in place of the inflictor’s stats).
  • Airborne Champions take half damage and are not inflicted with "Poison".
  • A Hazmat Suit negates the damage and the infliction entirely.
  • The 12 permanent spills on the map are vat leaks. Floods, drums, valves, ruptured pipes and Chemical Canisters create temporary spills; a temporary spill on an existing spill refreshes its duration instead of stacking.
Corroded Catwalk Special Tile
Footprint: 1 panel (+1, over the Great Vat) · Blocks: Nothing · HP: Integrity 3
  • Each time a non-airborne Champion enters a Corroded Catwalk panel, it loses 1 Integrity. Explosions and acid landing on it also remove 1.
  • At 0 Integrity the panel collapses once the current move resolves: any unit on it is Submerged, and the panel becomes part of the Great Vat for the rest of the battle.
