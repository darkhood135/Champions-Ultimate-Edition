Sonic

Sonic the Hedgehog

Striker

Free as the wind and twice as fast, Sonic the Hedgehog races across the world as its legendary blue blur — the fastest thing alive, and the one hero Dr. Eggman has never managed to stop.

Sonic answers to no one but the road ahead of him. A blue hedgehog with no home but wherever he happens to be running, he lives by a creed as simple as it is unshakable: go where you want, do what you know is right, and never let anyone take that freedom away — from you or from anyone else. It’s that last part that made him a hero. When the brilliant, egomaniacal Dr. Eggman began sealing the creatures of the world inside his robot army and paving its forests into factories, Sonic tore through the machines, set the animals free, and has been dismantling every scheme the doctor has dreamed up ever since. To most, he is cocky, impatient, and impossible to pin down — quick with a grin, a taunt, and a thumbs-up, and bored stiff by anything that asks him to slow down. But the swagger hides a fiercely loyal heart. He never leaves a friend behind, from Tails, the little brother who idolizes him, to Knuckles and Amy, and he has a habit of offering a second chance to the very rivals who came to bring him down. For all his love of the open road, he has never once failed to turn around when someone needed him.

His speed is the stuff of legend. Sonic breaks the sound barrier at a sprint, races up walls and around loops without breaking stride, and reacts fast enough to slip through a storm of fire as though it were standing still. He fights the way he runs: curling into a whirling ball of razor-sharp quills, he launches into a spin dash that shreds steel, and his homing attack carries him from foe to foe without his feet ever touching the ground. Gather all seven Chaos Emeralds, and their power transforms him into Super Sonic: blazing gold, untouchable, and fast enough to fly through the void of space. The one thing he has never learned to outrun is deep water, and he will happily insist to anyone who asks that he simply doesn’t need to. Speed isn’t a power to him so much as who he is. He runs for the joy of it: the wind, the open road, the next horizon. Every machine he wrecks and every cage he breaks open keeps the same promise: no one stays locked away while there’s still a blue blur on the horizon.

"Every world has its end. I know that's kind of sad, but... That's why we gotta live life to the fullest in the time we have. At least, that's what I figure."
