Memory

First Flight

Harry Potter Memory · The Wizarding World

The first flying lesson had begun, with first-years of all houses lining the yard. Harry, ecstatic to finally give the flying brooms a shot, braced himself for an eventful class. The fact they were organized by house meant he was lucky enough to be standing alongside Ron, Hermione and Neville rather than any of those jerks from Slytherin. Their brooms lay on the ground, ready to be called.

“Everyone, stick out your hand over your broom and say… UP!” Madam Hooch shouted, and every student gave it their shot. Harry’s broom immediately burst into his hand, like it was destined to be. Hermione’s broom weakly rolled over as she let out a light huff of disappointment. Neville’s broom, however, didn’t even move. As Harry glanced around, he noticed most students were stuck in a similar predicament to Neville.

As Hooch began discussing the mounting procedures, analyzing everyone’s form, she immediately began critiquing Malfoy, who sat alongside her two goons Crabbe and Goyle in the Slytherin section. “Malfoy, did you even listen to anything I just said? Correct the form, please.” Malfoy opened her mouth, stunned, to counter. “But, Madam Hooch, my father taught me this method and,” Hooch interjected, raising her voice this time. “Correct the form please, Malfoy.” Malfoy simply let out a light huff, her cheeks turning a slight pink, before adjusting. Harry couldn’t help but smirk to see her humbled.

Guess your father isn’t right about everything, Harry thought.

“Alright, when I blow this whistle, kick off the ground and give it your best shot. Rise four feet and then come back down. Do not go further and do not go until I whistle, please. Three… two…” As Madam Hooch was nearing the end of the countdown, Neville flew into the sky. Hooch looked up in dismay, sighing a little but clearly concerned. “Longbottom, please come back down this instant.” Despite her orders, Neville continued to fly higher and higher, likely reaching about twenty feet, shaking in fear. Harry saw his face of abject horror, alongside the height he had already reached, and found himself incredibly worried for Neville’s safety. In but a moment, Neville fell off of his broom and slammed onto the ground, letting out a screech of pain. Immediately after the fall, Harry heard Crabbe and Goyle withholding chuckles. He clenched his fist.

Hooch, white as a ghost, checked on Neville and found he only had a broken wrist. Relieved, but nevertheless concerned, she warned the class to stay put and escorted Neville to the hospital wing.

As soon as Neville and Hooch were gone, Malfoy’s goons broke into true laughter, echoing throughout the yard. Malfoy herself soon followed with light, awkward laughter. Their wickedness made him sick to his stomach.

Now without supervision, many from Slytherin immediately began mocking the other houses, criticizing their method and form. Malfoy, shockingly, stayed silent at first. It was only when Harry noticed her spot a little keepsake in the grass that her face lit up. She walked over, picking up Neville’s Remembrall from the ground. It shone in the light, reflecting the sun with glare and grabbing everyone’s attention, as Malfoy smirked with intention.

“This thing is as radiant as me… I think I’ll keep it.” She posed as proud as she usually does as her eyes darted to Harry, narrowing as though expecting a challenge. “You wouldn’t have anything to say about that, would you Potter?”

Everyone had their eyes on him, now, as whispers between his classmates began. Slytherin was clearly excited to see where this led.

“Give it back, Malfoy. That’s Neville’s, not yours.” Harry said simply, clutching his broom with purpose, awaiting Malfoy’s sure-to-come antics. She simply smiled nastily, eying down Harry like a viper. “Your friend should focus on other things. Like his future in flying.” She glanced at her Slytherin friends. “Especially considering he did so well the first time.” Her lackeys were the first to laugh as they all mocked Neville between each other once more. Some Slytherin, Harry noticed, remained uncomfortable throughout. As Malfoy turned her face towards Harry again, she grabbed her broom and yelled. “Come and get it, Potter!” Her face lighting with joy as she darted into the sky, taking Neville’s Remembrall with her. Harry immediately grabbed his broom as Hermione tried to grab his arm.

“Harry, she said not to move!” Hermione warned, as Harry simply turned to her with an uncaring nod before soaring into the sky, blood pounding in his ears. The broom was shockingly natural to control; fierce joy within him sparked amidst his anger, a realization that he finally found a calling. Everything else he’d had to struggle with or adapt to, but this? It felt freeing. Nevertheless, Harry focused his eyes on Malfoy as he rose to her level. Malfoy, utterly stunned, stared at Harry perplexed. Her malignantly happy expression now soured into a true glare. “Your form is off, Malfoy.” Harry nagged. He couldn’t help but smirk as Malfoy gritted her teeth.

She tried to take off again, darting with the Remembrall, but Harry was right on her trail. “Malfoy!” He called, “Hand it over, now!” He was right beside her now, keeping up with her easily. She must’ve realized her game was over as she tossed it in the air as though unsure what to do with it. Her eyes lit with a little bit of panic as Harry darted to the ground with the broom, swooping in like it was slow motion as he nabbed the Remembrall from the air before—

“POTTER!” Shouted Professor McGonagall, sprinting towards the group. Harry glanced over, Remembrall in hand. Malfoy had already positioned herself on the ground, alongside Crabbe and Goyle. Her face flushed with exhaustion as the two beside her snickered. She’d gone ahead and made herself look innocent. Malfoy glanced up at Harry briefly as Harry scowled at her, before she averted her eyes shamefully. Harry would be the one at fault; his friends tried to cover for him, but it was to no avail. McGonagall dragged Harry away as Harry feared for his school career. Only two weeks in and he’d already done it. To his surprise, however, she led him to a classroom. She knocked on the door as she glanced at Harry. Harry, expecting a face of anger was shocked to see that she looked… impressed. As someone answered the door, McGonagall redirected her gaze to them.

“I’d like to see Oliver Wood. I think we’ve found a new member for the team.”
