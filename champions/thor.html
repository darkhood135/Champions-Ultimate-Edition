Thor

Thor Odinson

Defender

The Asgardian God of Thunder and heir to the throne of Asgard, Thor Odinson wields the enchanted hammer Mjolnir in defense of the Nine Realms and the mortal world he has come to love.

Thor was born a god and had to learn how to be a hero. Prince of Asgard and son of the Allfather Odin, he was in his youth as arrogant as he was mighty — until his father, despairing of that pride, stripped him of his power and cast him into a mortal life to walk the earth as one of the fragile beings he'd been so quick to look down upon. That humbling remade him. The god who returned had learned compassion, patience, and the worth of a single mortal life, and he has spent the ages since as one of Midgard's most devoted protectors — a warrior who still thrills to the glory of a good battle, but who fights now for those who cannot fight for themselves. Honor is the marrow of him: he keeps his word, meets his foes head-on, and would sooner fall than betray the code of a true warrior. And woven through all of it is the one measure that defines him above every other — worthiness. His power is not simply his to command; he must earn it, every day, remaining the kind of man fit to wield it. A prince who could rule chose instead to serve, and a god who could demand worship asks only to stand between the innocent and the storm.

The power he commands is the raw fury of the heavens. As the God of Thunder, Thor summons lightning, gale, and tempest at will, and his godly strength and near-invulnerable Asgardian frame place him among the physically mightiest beings in all the realms. In his grip rests Mjolnir, a hammer forged in the heart of a dying star and bound by an enchantment so that only the worthy may lift it at all — and in worthy hands it calls down devastating bolts, looses blasts of energy, carries him through the sky like a hurled comet, and tears open the paths between worlds. Yet for all the thunder at his command, the truest source of his strength has never been the hammer. It is the worthiness that lets him raise it — the humility, honor, and love for others that a proud god learned the hard way, and has never once let go.

"All the power of the storm, from all the world, flows through my veins, and can be summoned by mine hammer at any time, wherever it is. A lightning storm in Japan? Mine. A hurricane off the coast of Barbados? Mine again. A brace of tornadoes in Kansas? Aye... mine. All that might, all that destructive force, mine to command. Channeled and guided through the mystic might of this hammer, guided right at thee!"

Thunder and Iron w/ Iron Man

Few friendships have weathered as much as Thor's with the Man of Iron. They stood together at the Avengers' founding and have fought side by side across the centuries since — the god of thunder and the mortal of science, forever needling one another over magic and machine, faith and pragmatism, yet bound by a respect that runs beneath every argument. It has not gone untested: Stark once profaned Thor's very image, building a soulless machine in his likeness that spilled a hero's blood, and the betrayal cut the thunder god to the core. That the bond survived even that is proof of how deep it runs — two shield-brothers who will quarrel until the end of days and stand together against anything that comes.

Honor of the Worthy w/ Captain America

To Thor, no honor he can bestow runs deeper than the one Steve Rogers has already earned — for the Captain is that rarest of mortals, a man of such pure and unbending conviction that he can lift Mjolnir itself, a feat gods and kings have failed. Thor deems him worthy in every sense of the word, and leads beside him not as a god among men but as one warrior of honor beside another. When the Captain speaks, the thunder god listens, and trusts that voice as readily as he would the counsel of any son of Asgard.

Children of the Pantheons w/ Wonder Woman

A god of Asgard rarely meets his equal, and Thor knew Diana for one the moment their paths first crossed. Son of Odin and champion of the gods of Olympus, they are divine warriors cut from the same ancient cloth — each raised among immortals, each sworn to shield the mortals below, each holding to a code of honor no battle could ever make them break. Between them lies the easy, unspoken understanding of two who carry the blood and burden of the old gods, and there are few in any realm Thor would sooner call comrade, or trust more surely at his side when the storm breaks.

Changelog

Balance changes since this Champion's release. Newest first.

v1.0.1 August 12, 2026
  • Nerf MAX HP reduced from 15,000 to 14,600.
  • Nerf MAG reduced from 300 to 250.
  • Nerf DEF reduced from 180 to 170.
  • Nerf RES reduced from 170 to 160.
  • Nerf Bring the Hammer Down (Honor of the Worthy synergy): the 50% chance to Stun enemies within 4 panels no longer benefits from Thor's infliction-chance increases, and the guaranteed Stun within 1 panel is now specified as 1 turn.

As the more offensive of his lane's two Defenders, Thor was outpacing Wonder Woman on durability, hybrid damage, and utility all at once. These stat trims — made without touching any of his abilities — bring his frame in line with hers while keeping his identity as the hybrid control-wall intact. The Bring the Hammer Down limitation stops his self-buffed infliction chances from turning the combo's wide-area Stun into near-guaranteed mass hard-CC.
