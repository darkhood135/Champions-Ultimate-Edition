Goku

Son Goku · Kakarot

Berserker

A Saiyan infant sent to conquer Earth, only to be raised as one of its gentlest sons, Goku stands as the planet's mightiest protector — a warrior who fights not to destroy, but to defend.

Goku was bred by a warrior race to be a weapon, and became instead the kindest man on Earth by pure accident. Sent to a distant world to conquer it, he struck his head as an infant and lost every trace of the ruthless purpose written into his blood, growing up in the mountains as a cheerful, guileless boy who knew only how to be good. When his brother finally came to drag that heritage back into the light, Goku learned in a single afternoon that he was an alien, a Saiyan, and the last hope of a planet that had no idea what was coming for it — and without hesitation he chose Earth, his family, and his friends over the blood he'd just discovered. He is a fighter to his very core, alive in a way he never otherwise is when he faces an opponent who can push him to his limit, but that love of the battle is bound to something rarer: he fights only to protect, and there is no price he won't pay to do it. He gave up his own life to stop his brother, clung to an enemy so a friend could land the killing blow, and even standing over the Saiyan prince who'd butchered everyone he loved, he chose mercy. That is the paradox of him — the deadliest man on the planet, carrying its purest heart.

Everything he can do, he earned through a lifetime of relentless training and one hard death. Goku is a peerless martial artist whose strength, speed, and durability tower far beyond any ordinary man's, and he bends his own life energy — his ki — to soar through the air, blur into afterimages, and unleash the wave of blue fire that is his signature Kamehameha. Slain against his brother, he trained beyond the grave under a master among the dead and returned with two techniques that changed everything: the Kaio-ken, which multiplies his every attribute in a searing red aura at the cost of tearing his own body apart, and the Spirit Bomb, a devastating sphere gathered from the borrowed energy of every living thing around him. But raw power was never the whole of his strength. It's that he rises every single time he is knocked down, reaches past his limits when the people behind him need him to, and simply refuses — cheerfully, stubbornly — to ever stop trying.

"You hate losing. You're not used to fighting someone stronger than you!"

Rivalry to Last a Lifetime w/ Vegeta

Vegeta is the most dangerous thing Goku has ever faced — a Saiyan prince who slaughtered his friends without a second thought and pushed him past every limit he had, dragging out the Kaio-ken and the Spirit Bomb just to survive the day. And yet, bloodied and victorious with his enemy lying helpless before him, Goku let him live. Part of it was the goodness that never quite leaves him; part of it was the warrior in him, thrilling at the discovery of an opponent who could genuinely test his limits. Where any sane man would see a monster that needed putting down, Goku saw the fight of his life only just beginning — and a rival, however vicious, worth meeting again someday at his very best.

Sons of Dying Worlds w/ Superman

Two sons of dying worlds, sent to Earth as infants and raised by humble hands into its gentlest guardians — Goku and Superman are very nearly the same story told twice, and each recognized it in the other at once. But where the Man of Steel carries his power like a solemn duty, Goku can't help seeing the finest sparring partner he's ever laid eyes on: the one man who might be able to cut loose against him completely and still be standing at the end. Superman has no interest in trading blows with a friend, and Goku respects that even as he never quite stops asking — because to a warrior like him, the truest compliment he knows how to pay a man is wanting, more than anything, to find out just how strong he really is.

Changelog

Balance changes since this Champion's release. Newest first.

v1.0.1 July 11, 2026
  • Nerf MAX HP reduced 12,000 → 9,200.
  • Nerf King Kai's Training (Kaioken Core Passive) — HP restored on defeating an enemy reduced 20% → 12%. Transformation extension and EN restore unchanged.
  • Nerf Quiet Wrath (Kaioken Special 4) — per-hit damage reduced 170% → 140% ATK (840% total across 6 hits, down from 1020%).

As released, Goku carried the third-highest HP pool in the roster on a role — Berserker — defined by trading survivability for damage, and his Kaioken form's HP costs were meant to be that trade. In practice they weren't: King Kai's Training refunded 20% HP per kill on top of raised regen, so in any multi-enemy fight his health climbed while he dealt top-tier damage and held the transformation indefinitely, and the intended risk never surfaced. Lowering the base pool and, more importantly, the per-kill refund turns Kaioken into a genuine countdown — his health now trends downward while transformed, so the big pool reads as fuel for a few decisive turns rather than a wall, and his revival and endurance passives become the safety net they were designed to be rather than redundant insurance. Quiet Wrath was additionally the single largest per-ability hit in the roster; the trim keeps it a premier Berserker nuke without it exceeding every dedicated damage dealer's ceiling. His burst identity and transformation economy are otherwise intact.
