Memory

Drawing the Line

Harry Potter Memory · The Wizarding World

As the Hogwarts Express pushed on ahead, Harry found himself in the same compartment as a fellow student named Ron Weasley.

“I still can’t believe you’ve never heard of Quidditch!”

Ron was going on and on about Quidditch, a sport Harry had never even heard of only hours before. The conversation wasn’t unwelcome, of course, and was quite a relief from all the stress of the trip (and from the torturous life with the Dursleys back at home). This was unfamiliar territory for Potter; He’d never heard of golden snitches or flying broomsticks or anything of the like. The idea, even at the base level, was enough to get his heart pumped with excitement.

Wow, he thought, that must be quite the game.

Ron had only just been getting into the depths of the game, when they heard the opening of the compartment door.

Two boys and a girl entered; One of which, he recognized immediately: the pale girl from Madam Malkin’s robe shop. Her eyes scanned both of them before widening a little at Harry, recognition crossing her face. She was certainly looking at him with greater intent than she had back in Diagon Alley (if she had even looked at him at all).

“Is it true? You’re Harry Potter?” She exclaimed, a strange bit of excitement rolling off of her tongue. “Everyone’s been saying that Harry Potter is in this compartment.”

Harry nodded, a little uncomfortable at his own sudden popularity. “Yes, it is.” He scanned the two beside her; One stood with a mean grimace on his face with a clear ill intent immediately. The other stood larger, a little lazier, seemingly uncaring at the situation. Harry, waiting for an answer from this girl (who was now staring at him intently without words), opened his mouth to question. “So, who’s…?”

“Oh, right… This is Crabbe and this is Goyle.” The girl uttered, carelessly motioning to both of them. “And, most importantly, I’m Malfoy. Dracaena Malfoy.” She tossed her hair aside in a sweeping motion, putting her hands on her hips as she stood with an unbelievable amount of pride. Harry didn’t like it one bit.

Ron coughed a little, his mouth morphing in the corners into a slight grin despite his attempts to hide it. Malfoy immediately redirected her gaze from Harry to Ron. “Is my name funny to you?” Annoyance creeped across her face as she looked at him coldly. Ron, now unable to hide it, let out a slight snigger. “Sorry mate, but that motion was…” Ron went into laughter now as Malfoy’s ears began to turn a shade of pink, clearly embarrassed. Crabbe’s eyes, the larger one, suddenly flashed with recognition. Crabbe put his hand on Malfoy’s shoulder as he pointed at Ron.

“Ah, Malfoy… this one’s a Weasley.” Crabbe snorted as though Ron were mere filth. “Those blokes are the red-headed, broke peasants… none of which are worth the effort. No wonder he can’t recognize your elegance.” Harry, already done with these people, glanced back at Ron (whose laughter ceased rather quickly) as Ron’s eyes quickly filled with rage. Crabbe and Goyle started laughing, their laughs sounding more like barks of dying dogs, as Malfoy momentarily joined in. “Ah, I see… well, that doesn’t mean much coming from you, does it Weasley?” Malfoy shook her head before once again redirecting her gaze to Potter. “You’ll soon find out who’s worth your time, Potter. You don’t want to go making friends with the wrong sort. But don’t worry, I’ll graciously be your guide to this place, and maybe even be your friend. How’s that sound?” She held out her hand with complete confidence, waiting for Harry to take it. Harry simply met her eyes, trailed to her hand, then back to her face.

“I think I can figure it out on my own, thanks.” He said coolly. Malfoy, for a moment, appeared a little stunned. Within a moment, her face began to morph into a dull pink, her breathing turning a little shaky. Nevertheless, she played it off, and shook her head in disbelief. “Unbelievable. I offer you a grand opportunity and you can’t even take it… all for the sake of a Weasley.” She fired a sneer at Ron. “You hang around people like him and you’ll never make it in this school.”

Ron stood up, shaking with anger now. “Say anything more about my family.” Ron said, his face as red as his hair. Crabbe, seizing the opportunity, jumped in front of Malfoy. “You’re going to fight us now?” His face lit up with a contemptible grin. Harry, glancing to his right and seeing his new friend targeted, leapt to his feet as well, turning to face the oaf.

“Unless you get out now.” Harry said, more bravely than he felt, as Crabbe and Goyle towered over them both. Behind Crabbe, Malfoy stared at Harry like something had been stolen from her. Something about that stare put Harry off a little, but he had no time to focus on her. He quickly dismissed it, redirecting his focus to the threat in front of him.

Goyle now stepped in, carrying the same sense of entitlement. “But we don’t feel like leaving yet, do we? We already ate all of our food back at our compartment, and it looks like you still have a few. Those will be going to her, thanks.” His hand reached towards the Chocolate Frogs near Ron as Ron nearly launched a fist at him. Before that, however, Goyle leapt back with a yell.

Ron’s rat, Scabbers, hung off of Goyle’s finger, teeth sinking deep into Goyle’s knuckle with an intense force. Goyle shook and shook his hand until Scabbers finally went flying into the window, Ron catching him with care. Crabbe and Goyle slinked back, Goyle nearly crying from pain, as they darted from the compartment. Malfoy, shocked at her sudden loss of protection, let one last comment fly as she booked it down the hall. “This isn’t over, Potter! No one embarrasses a Malfoy and gets away with it!”

After that mess was done with, Hermione stepped back into the compartment. “What’s been going on?” Harry, simply relieved that the others were gone, shrugged his shoulders.
