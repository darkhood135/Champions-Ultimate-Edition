Dark Rider

Shadow's Motorcycle

Booster

With a snarl of its engine, the Dark Rider tears onto the battlefield, black and crimson as its master: a heavy, low-slung motorcycle that Shadow has never once needed and rides anyway. The Ultimate Life Form could leave it in the dust on his own two feet, but speed was never the point. The machine lets him fight on the move, hands free for a Chaos Spear or a weapon, its armored frame taking the punishment that would otherwise land on him while he carves through the enemy line at full throttle. There is a cold, deliberate style to it, too: the roar that announces him long before he arrives, the skid that stops him exactly where he means to be, the unhurried way he swings down from the seat once the fight is done. Shadow would deny it has anything to do with image. Everyone else knows better.

"Where's that damn fourth Chaos Emerald?"

Passive Abilities
Riding in Style Passive 1
  • The summoner can ride and dismount from this summon at will.
  • The rider cannot perform attacks while riding (excluding Pistol Fire).
  • The rider of this summon, when moving, will consume the summon's STA first (until depleted).
  • The rider dismounts automatically upon transforming.
Metal Exterior Passive 2
  • 60% of ATK damage directed at the rider will instead be directed at this summon.
  • 30% of MAG damage directed at the rider will instead be directed at this summon.
  • This summon restores 5 STA per turn.
  • Cannot receive healing or buffs from any source.
  • Expires after 2 turns.
  • Deals 200% ATK damage to any ally or enemy within 1 panel of this mount if it is destroyed prior to expiration.
  • The damage upon destruction does not affect Shadow.
