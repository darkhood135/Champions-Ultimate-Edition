Patronus (Malfoy)

Peacock Patronus

Booster

For most of her life, Dracaena had nothing pure enough to conjure one — a Patronus is born of a happy memory held without a shadow over it, and hers were all tangled up in fear, legacy, and the lies she’d been raised to mistake for truth. It was only when she finally let herself look toward the one thing she’d spent years refusing to admit she felt — a love fragile and sincere and wholly her own — that the light at last had somewhere to come from. At her word, a peacock of brilliant silver bursts into the air, its tail unfurling in a great radiant fan: the old sigil of House Malfoy, so long a mark of cold and brittle pride, reborn now as something luminous and alive. It sweeps across the battlefield scattering despair and darkness alike, every feather blazing with the warmth she was once taught to be ashamed of. For Dracaena, casting it is less a spell than a confession — proof, written in light, that she has finally learned where to look for the happiness that was always just out of her reach.

"I've spent my life staring within the abyss of sins, feeding on lies as if it's my birthright. But once, I looked the other way. I gazed upon a love so fragile, so sincere. Though I never wanted to admit it... unknowingly, it stares back. Now I know where to look. Fly high... Expecto Patronum!"
