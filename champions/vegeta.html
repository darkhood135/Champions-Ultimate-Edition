Vegeta

Prince Vegeta IV

Blaster

The last prince of a merciless warrior race, Vegeta descends upon Earth as its would-be conqueror — a Saiyan of monstrous power who kneels to no one and believes, to his core, that the strong were born to rule the weak.

Vegeta is what Goku might have become had he never lost the cruelty bred into his blood. Prince of the Saiyans, raised in a culture that measured worth in conquest and body counts, he carries his royal lineage like a birthright to the entire universe — and his pride is not one trait among many but the whole of him, the engine behind every act of savagery he commits without a flicker of remorse. He came to Earth chasing the Dragon Balls and the immortality that would make his dominion eternal, and he treats every life beneath his own as an obstacle or an amusement, cutting down even his fellow Saiyan the instant the man ceased to be useful to him. Mercy, to Vegeta, is a disease that infects the weak. Yet for all his monstrousness, what truly defines his time on Earth is the wound he never saw coming: humbled for the first time in his life by a low-born Saiyan he'd dismissed as trash and a rabble of lesser creatures, bloodied and forced to flee, his body will heal long before his pride does. He does not retreat broken. He retreats insulted — and swearing, with everything he has, that it will never happen twice.

The power backing that arrogance is, at this moment, greater than anything Earth's defenders have ever faced. Vegeta's strength, speed, and ferocity tower over every warrior sent against him, his mastery of ki loosing devastating blasts like the violet torrent of his Galick Gun — a beam that can answer even Goku's Kamehameha head-on. And when brute force isn't enough, he unveils the Saiyan race's most terrible weapon: hurling a sphere of light into the sky as a false moon, he swells into a Great Ape, a towering monster with his every ounce of power multiplied tenfold, laying waste to everything beneath him. He is defeated on Earth, but only barely, and only together — and as his pod streaks back into the stars, the most dangerous truth about the Saiyan Prince is that this humiliation is not the end of him. It is the beginning of an obsession that will make him stronger than anyone yet dares imagine.

"We're going to wait for him to show his cowardly face...then we'll make him watch while we kill his son and all of his friends."

Rivalry to Last a Lifetime w/ Goku

To Vegeta, there is no insult in all the universe like Kakarot. A low-class Saiyan, born beneath him by every measure a prince is raised to respect, somehow clawed his way high enough to humble the royal bloodline itself — and that wound festers in Vegeta's pride like nothing ever has. He does not thank Goku for sparing his life; he despises him for it, for the pity buried in the mercy. From the moment his pod tears away from Earth, every waking hour bends toward a single purpose: to grow strong enough to wipe out that humiliation, and prove at last that the Prince of all Saiyans kneels to no one — least of all to Kakarot.

Changelog

Balance changes since this Champion's release. Newest first.

v1.0.1 July 11, 2026
  • Nerf Ki Blast Barrage — cooldown added: None → 1 turn (an unintended leftover from Iron Man's Repulsor Blast; the ability was always intended to carry a cooldown). Damage, width and Sting unchanged.
  • Buff Great Ape Slam (ULTIMATUM 1) — damage increased 700% → 1000% ATK and area of effect increased 3 → 4 panels.
  • Nerf MAX HP reduced 8,200 → 7,800.
  • Rework Beam Clash (Goku synergy) — corrected wording typos ("eachother" → "each other," "inbetween" → "in between"). No functional change.

Ki Blast Barrage shipped with no cooldown — a copy-paste holdover from Iron Man's Repulsor Blast — which, combined with its very large footprint and Vegeta's kill-fueled energy economy, let it be chain-cast far beyond intent. Its intended 1-turn cooldown restores that pacing while keeping the wide-line identity intact. Great Ape Slam was the opposite problem: as an ATK-scaling ULTIMATUM on a MAG-focused Champion it landed below his own specials and well below his reserve ULT, making it a trap pick; the higher multiplier and radius make the primary ULTIMATUM a genuine payoff again. The small HP trim nudges him toward the frailer end the Blaster role implies while keeping him just above Iron Man in the two-Blaster ordering. His airborne damage engine, snowball economy, and the Goku synergy are otherwise untouched.
