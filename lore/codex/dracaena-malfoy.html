Overview

Dracaena Malfoy is a character from The Wizarding World — heir to a proud, pure-blooded house, and, against every expectation set for her, the one thing her legacy never intended: her own person.

A gifted and precise duelist, she fights not through brute force but through resourcefulness and a razor’s composure, matching far stronger opponents through sheer cleverness and a refusal to yield an inch. This entry collects her story across the three eras, her relationships and personality, how this version departs from her source, and the Memories anchored to her.
