Memory

Detention

Harry Potter Memory · The Wizarding World

Harry, Neville and Hermione found their way to Argus Filch in the castle’s entrance hall, disappointment and dread filling their minds. What would a detention look like? Where would they go? What would they do? Harry’s mind tried to piece together the possibilities as he feared for his school career. Looking at Hermione, it was clear she was no better off, if not significantly worse; Neville, similarly, wore a face of pure panic.

To Harry’s surprise and dismay, Malfoy approached from a few feet away with a huff, her arms crossed and her stance reserved. She didn’t look nearly as scared as they did, only appearing to be inconvenienced.

“Ah, about time you showed up, Malfoy. Come on, kids. It’s time to face the music.” Filch growled, a leer mounting his face as he led the group outside and across the dark grounds. “You know, we used to have truly painful punishments. Maybe you’ll face one of them if you try to run off…” Filch let out a barking laugh as Harry, hoping he was joking, gulped. Things were certainly bad if Filch was enjoying himself.

After what felt like ages of uncertainty and anxiousness, Harry saw the lit frame of Hagrid’s hut amidst the cloudy sky. Part of him was immediately relieved at the idea that this would just be any other day with Hagrid. But as Hagrid called from a distance, his voice booming through the air, he figured this wouldn’t quite be so simple. Beside him sat a large dog, menacing in appearance.

“Welcome, students! You ready ter get started?” They definitely weren’t; But Filch would be sure to throw them through the ringer if they objected. Harry nodded, bracing for their task, as he glanced over at the others. Malfoy appeared to still be disinterested. Hermione wore a brave face and Neville, visibly shaking, looked like he’d rather be back on a broom. Hagrid continued as Filch departed.

“Your task today, kids… is ter enter the Forbidden Forest!” Before he could say another word, Malfoy let out a startled cry. Hagrid and the others redirected their gaze to her as she started to back away, her eyes now reading pure panic. She shook her head rapidly. “No, no, no, we can’t. Please, we can’t! There’s… there’s monsters in there! And—” Hermione, despite her own fear, let out a little snicker.

“Well, you shoulda thought of that beforehand then! You want ter stay in Hogwarts, you betta listen.” Hagrid grumbled, scratching his beard. “Look, I’ll be joining ye; I’ve got my boy Fang and my ol’ crossbow ere. Fang may be a coward, but he’s fearsome when he needs ter be.” He picked up a giant crossbow, nearly the size of one of them, wielding it easily with one arm.

“That doesn’t matter!” Malfoy argued, her fear abundant in her voice. “I… I thought we’d be having normal detention! My father wouldn’t like this one bit, he’d… he’d protect me if he was here! He’d get you in trouble!” She began biting her lip, shaking a little with her arms to her sides in an upset pout. Hagrid, patiently, responded. “Listen, for the last time, we’re just taking a trip inside. We’re going ter be fine as long as ye follow my orders. Consider it a field trip of sorts. Filch is just tryna scare yer.” He shook his head. “Besides, what is copying some lines good ter anyone? We’re going hunting for a creature tonight! Specifically, whatever’s been killing the unicorns.” Harry breathed in slowly, trying to grasp the severity of what they were hunting for.

“S-so… we’re trying to track a monster?” Neville began, him and Malfoy nigh-identically frightened. “Ye could call it that, I suppose.” Hagrid muttered. “But do as I say and we’ll all be alright. Understand me?” The group nodded. “Alright then, listen ere. That shiny stuff on the ground over ere? Unicorn blood. That’s our sign it struck tonight. We’re going to move tergether as one, ye hear me? I’m not sure what it is in these woods, so stick close, and don’t do anything reckless!” He let Fang take the reins as he directed them towards the woods. Hermione followed first and Neville trailed behind her. Harry, beginning to follow, heard Malfoy trailing far in the distance. As they got closer and closer to the forest, her steps started to become quicker-paced and frantic before she finally was side-by-side with Harry, her eyes glistening with tears and her teeth chattering. Harry tried to ignore her; He faced forward, fighting all of his urges to flee as they pursued Hagrid.

As long as Hagrid is here, Harry thought, we’ll be alright… hopefully.

Suddenly, Malfoy grabbed hold of Harry’s robe with a fist. Harry, startled, reflexively tried to tug it away from her as she held on, tightening her grip even further. Confused, he looked at her directly. Her fear, which he previously would have gained some amusement from, was no longer something he could laugh at. Tears poured down her cheeks and she sniffled intensely as she tried her best to hold it together. Her feet barely appeared to be able to walk as they dragged themselves weakly after the group. She appeared so helpless and vulnerable that he couldn’t help but feel a little awful even despite what she’d done thus far. Refusing to look him in the eye, her gaze averted, still holding onto his robe.

She’s just scared, Harry thought, and she has no one here.

Harry let her hold onto him, tossing aside his grudges to lend a hand. In truth, he was likely just as scared as her.

After a few minutes of walking, the forest showed its true colors. The intimidating yet contained trees from the border of the forest no longer showed themselves. The new trees seemed to have minds of their own, branches dangling towards the children; Dark, dreary and intentional. The distant sounds around them grew in both quantity and volume. Birds, insects and who knows what else let their cries fly in the belly of the woods. A few small spiders darted across the ground, each one causing a yelp from Malfoy.

“Halt!” Hagrid ordered, Fang stopping dead in his tracks. The kids gasped and did as they were told as Malfoy lunged to Harry’s arm in terror. Hagrid readied his crossbow ahead before throwing out a warning. “If yer can hear me, show yerself.” Where the crossbow centered, at a patch of woods in the distance, a half-human half-horse stepped out with confidence. Neville gasped as Malfoy clutched Harry even closer. Harry recognized this creature; a centaur. Hermione had gone on a long tangent about their history in the Gryffindor common room.

Hagrid lowered his crossbow. “Ah, it’s just yer, Ronan.” He muttered, petting Fang (who was now quivering). “Indeed it is, Hagrid.” The creature, Ronan, responded. “…Were you going to shoot me?” Hagrid shrugged. “Well, can’t be too careful out ere. There’s something that’s been killing the unicorns, y’know. Ye notice anything strange in these ere woods?” Ronan shook his head. “Can’t say I have. Well, except for how Mars looks tonight.” Ronan stared up at the sky for a moment before turning his attention to the students by Hagrid’s side. “What’re these ones doing out here this late?” Hagrid glanced over the group, his eyes stopping on Malfoy and Harry for a moment with a raised eyebrow. “They’re ere for detention. I’m ere to keep em safe.” He scratched his chin, pondering for a moment as he redirected his glance to Ronan. “Yer sure yer haven’t noticed anything strange?” Ronan shook his head with a final rejection as Hagrid nodded. “Alright then. I guess we’ll be seeing yer then, Ronan.”

Ronan nodded, waltzing away with his horse hooves as he waved goodbye to the students. “Study well, children. And be safe.” Stunned by the encounter, the four of them looked between each other. Before Hermione or Neville could catch Malfoy’s clinging, she retracted her arm speedily. “Let’s continue on, then.” Hagrid asserted.

A few minutes passed before Malfoy suddenly stopped dead in her tracks. Harry turned to face her, his eyes darting between her and Hagrid as the two groups gained more and more distance, Hagrid looking over his shoulder at Harry. “Malfoy, we need to,” Harry began, concern in him growing as she appeared completely oblivious to his speech. Her eyes darted around the forest, twitching at a level that couldn’t be healthy. Harry, unwilling to leave her behind, stayed with her as he offered her a weak assertion. “It’ll be okay, Malfoy. We’ll be okay. But we have to move.” Something about Harry’s voice got through this time as Malfoy, with chattering teeth and a moment of hesitation, let out a dull nod. Then suddenly, before either even took another step, another halting boom from Hagrid echoed through the forest. He was barely visible now; Appearing only through the cracks in the trees.

“Arry! Malfoy! Get over ere! We found something!” Hagrid shouted, a nervous tone cracking a little through the strong front. Malfoy, now ready to bolt, began to tug on Harry’s robe as all of a sudden an intense pain shot through his skull. A brutal, throbbing pain echoed as he let out a pained cry. He’d never felt anything like it before, a pain so intense it began to void all of his surroundings and left him on his knees. Malfoy retracted her arm in surprise.

From the corner of his eye, Harry saw it; A hooded figure, first darting through the trees, now looking at them with purpose. The creature’s face remained almost entirely hidden beyond a sinister scowl which the little bit of light shone upon. Before Harry could even react, it began moving towards them at an ungodly speed; Harry, in an instant, was up, despite the pain. No time to think. Wand drawn, he shielded Malfoy with his arm, ready to clash with the beast if need be. Malfoy had other intentions; Right as Harry made contact with her, she ran screaming in the other direction.

Now alone, Harry tried to stand his ground; But now he had no assistance. The creature got closer and closer as time seemed to go in slow motion.

What spell do I use? What spell do I use? What do I—

A shot from Hagrid’s crossbow nailed the creature in the shoulder, a demonic screech releasing from the villain’s throat. It redirected, focused now on getting away as Hagrid, yelling, sprinted towards Harry.

“Arry! Arry! Thank god yere okay! Where’s Malfoy?” Hermione likewise, ran to Harry. “Harry, are you okay?!” Harry, still stunned, looked at Hagrid with a weak but terrified stare. “I’m okay, I’m okay. Is that the creature you guys saw?” Harry’s head still pounded something fierce, now fading slowly. “Not just the creature.” Hagrid began. “It was draining the blood of another unicorn, the poor thing. Yer lucky I had my eyes on yer. What were yer thinking staying back like that?” Harry, unsure how to explain it, simply shrugged. “It… was Malfoy. She… she went that way. We need to find her before anything happens.” Hagrid, nodded, approval in his voice as he agreed. “Just like you, Arry. I’m proud of yer boy.” He patted Harry on the back. “Alright kids, follow me.” Rallying the group, he directed them with a point towards where Malfoy went. “We’re going to be okay. I promise ye. Malfoy will be alright, on my ere heart and soul.” Harry doubted Hermione or Neville cared much about that last part.

As the group pursued where Malfoy ran off, they began to get worried. They hadn’t heard any sign of Malfoy or the creature. Suddenly, another noise came from some bushes nearby. Hagrid steadied his crossbow in an instant, directing it at the noise.

“It’s alright, Hagrid. I’ve got the girl.” A younger voice, as another centaur emerged. Malfoy was standing close to his side, still shaking. “Ah! Firenze! Thank yer.” He dropped his crossbow as Malfoy ran to the group in tears. She dashed into Harry, grabbing hold of his arm with an even greater intensity than she had prior. This time, Hermione and Neville witnessed it firsthand. A little embarrassed, Harry glanced at Hermione, who wore an expression of pure confusion. Neville appeared a little bothered. Harry offered Malfoy a light pat as Hagrid let out a light chuckle. “Ah, this one likes you, Arry!”

“Shut up!” Malfoy rebutted immediately. “He’s my bodyguard, a worthless peasant.” She let go of him now, her pride overpowering her fear. “None of you better say anything about this! Or my father will hear about it! Especially you two, Granger and Longbottom!” A moment of silence as they all stared at Malfoy before Firenze began, Hermione scowling. “Alright, well… I have news, Hagrid. The beast… we believe it’s… him. Incarnate again.” Harry, Hagrid and Hermione gasped, immediately realizing. “You mean we’re dealing with…?” “Yes.” Firenze responded in an instant, concern mounting his human face. “I know no other creature who would willingly kill a unicorn; One who would curse his life just to do so. If we’re correct, he’s seeking to regain his full power.” Hagrid shook his head as he turned to the group, eying them all individually. “Then we ought to leave ere, kids. Come on. Thank yer, Firenze.” Firenze nodded, and as the group began to depart, a new voice echoed through the trees. Hagrid, always alert, swung the crossbow.

“Firenze, you fool!” The hooves of another centaur neared with zero fear, as a larger one made his appearance. “You go blabbing your mouth about the secrets shared between us?!” Ronan stepped out as well, joining the side of the other. “He’s only trying to help them, Bane.” “It means nothing. Firenze, come with us now.” Hagrid grumbled as Firenze let out a weak nod. Before Firenze departed, he glanced at Harry.

“Good luck, Harry Potter. May the planets read different in time; Even we’ve made mistakes, before.” With that vague allusion, he was gone. Hagrid cracked his shoulders as he rallied the group once more. “Well, let’s get back, then.”

As they began to walk back, Harry questioned Hagrid. “Hagrid, what did he mean about a ‘cursed life’?” He adjusted his glasses in anticipation as Hagrid momentarily hesitated, unsure whether to tell him the full picture. “Well, Arry… one who drinks the blood of a unicorn is considered cursed because, though they may survive, they sacrificed an innocent creature to do so. They sought power above all else with no care for the sanctity of the creature.” Hagrid shook his head. “Truly monstrous, indeed.”

Before long, they were back at Hagrid’s hut. Harry, exhausted, looked between his classmates. Hermione and Neville looked equally drained. Malfoy looked more embarrassed than anything. “Alright, kids. Now’s when we depart from ere. I’ll let Filch know yer lot did yer work. More eventful than you thought, eh?” They redirected their gaze to Malfoy as she let out a scoff. “It could’ve been worse.” She redirected her gaze to Harry now. “You were a mediocre bodyguard at best, Potter.” She tossed her hair again as Hermione groaned. Hagrid shook his head. “Aight, aight. Come with me, now.”

They reached the door of the castle entrance. Hagrid waved them goodbye and they all prepared to head back to their common rooms. “I’m serious, Potter! You were a tool, nothing more.” Malfoy darted off through the door with a disapproving glance behind her as Harry stayed put, upset and embarrassed. Hermione and Neville looked at Harry as Hermione opened her mouth to talk. “You’ve got to tell us what happened when we get back. I’ve never seen Malfoy so scared!” Harry blinked and rubbed his eyes a little.

“Not tonight. I… I can’t even think right now.” As Harry adjusted his glasses, he couldn’t help but notice a faint glimmer of unicorn blood in the grass beside them.
