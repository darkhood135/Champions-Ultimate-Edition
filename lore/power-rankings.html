Power rankings

Every character with a Power overview, on one shared multiversal scale. Each category runs from 1 to 10, and a rating means the same thing in every universe: a wizard’s 3 and a Saiyan’s 3 are the same 3. Click a column to sort, or filter by universe. These are story ratings, separate from gameplay stats.

The table fills in automatically as character articles are written. Each character’s ratings live in one place, the Archive’s page index, so their article and this table always agree.
