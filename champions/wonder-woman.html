Wonder Woman

Diana of Themyscira

Defender

Princess of the immortal Amazons, shaped by the gods and sent forth from a hidden paradise into a world at war, Diana fights as Wonder Woman — a warrior of peerless skill and an unshakable champion of peace, truth, and compassion.

Wonder Woman is the rarest thing a warrior can be: one who fights for peace and means it. Raised on the hidden island of Themyscira among the immortal Amazons — a sisterhood of women forged in battle yet devoted to wisdom and love — Diana grew up the finest fighter of a race with millennia to perfect the art, and then chose to leave paradise behind to carry her people's ideals into a broken world of strangers. That is the paradox at the heart of her, and the source of everything remarkable about her. She leads with compassion where others lead with fear or force, seeking always to redeem an enemy before she defeats one, to understand before she strikes, to see in humanity the best of what it could be and hold it to that standard. Yet she is no naïf and no pacifist. Where Superman and Batman bind themselves with absolute rules, Diana carries a warrior's clear-eyed resolve: when mercy has been offered and refused and lives hang in the balance, she will do what must be done without flinching. Truth is sacred to her, cowardice and cruelty are the only things she cannot abide, and she loves a world that has rarely earned it — fiercely, and on purpose.

The power to back that conviction was gifted by the gods themselves. Diana's strength, speed, and durability stand among the mightiest on Earth, enough to trade blows with beings that could level cities, and she carries a lifetime of Amazonian training that makes her perhaps the single greatest hand-to-hand and armed combatant alive. At her hip coils the Lasso of Truth, an unbreakable golden cord that compels any it binds to speak only what is real; upon her wrists sit the indestructible Bracelets that turn aside bullets and blasts with a flick; and in her hands, tiara, sword, and shield become extensions of a will honed over a lifetime of discipline. But her deadliest weapons were never the gods' gifts. It is the compassion she wields with the very same hands that can end any battle — the unbreakable belief that even in a world of war, love is the stronger and braver thing.

"For there is at least one truth, and both of us know it. It's one of the things that calls us to each other, despite everything. Because someday our battles will end. And there would be no greater tragedy... than facing that end alone."

Hope Made Flesh w/ Superman

Diana and Clark are the two great hopeful hearts of their world — each blessed with power beyond mortal reckoning, each an outsider who could have stood apart from humanity and instead chose, wholly and freely, to love it. She came to mankind as an ambassador and he was raised as one of their own, yet they arrived at the very same devotion, and in one another they found the rarest thing of all: an equal who leads not with fear or force but with compassion. There is no one whose faith in a better world Diana trusts more completely, and no one she would sooner stand beside to hold the line for it.

Two Kinds of Strength w/ Batman

Diana has fought beside few mortals who ever truly earned an Amazon's respect as a warrior, and Batman is chief among them — his will, his discipline, and his refusal to yield are things she honors without reservation. What she cannot make peace with is the darkness he has chosen to live inside, the compassion he denies himself as though it were a weakness rather than the greatest strength of all. She is the warmth he keeps forever at arm's length, and he the shadow she wishes she could coax back into the light; they have stood on opposite sides when honor demanded it, and trusted each other absolutely in every fight that mattered.

Changelog

Balance changes since this Champion's release. Newest first.

v1.0.1 July 11, 2026
  • Rework Power of Olympus (Core Passive) — the random self-buff is now defined and bounded. Previously "30% chance to apply a random rare buff" (undefined pool, ambiguous trigger); now applies one of Might, Overflow, Pinpoint or Light at random, once per ability.
  • Rework United Sisters (Reserve ULTIMATUM) — buff target corrected from "Amazonian" to "Amazon" to match the link; previously it applied to no one.
  • Nerf Rallying Cry (Captain America synergy) — the +1-turn nullification extension now requires all allies to be affected and standing (was 4 or more allies).

Power of Olympus was the kit's least-defined and highest-ceiling line. An unspecified "random rare buff" applied per damage instance could pile several strong buffs — including Energy Barrier's negation and guaranteed critical hits — onto an already-elite tank in a single multi-hit turn. Naming a four-buff pool and firing it once per ability keeps the "random Olympian blessing" fantasy while making the effect legible and bounded, and removes the compounding-durability outcomes. United Sisters referenced a link spelling ("Amazonian") that does not exist, so it buffed nobody; corrected to match the "Amazon" link. Rallying Cry's extended nullification window now demands the full team be present and alive rather than any four allies, tightening the strongest team-defensive grant in the roster while leaving its base effect — the shield-and-sword wall the bond is built around — intact.
