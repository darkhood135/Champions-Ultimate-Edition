Dragon Ball

Saiyans, Dragon Balls, and gods of destruction.

Dragon Ball follows from a boy with a monkey’s tail to a mortal who mastered Ultra Instinct. In this continuity Daima takes place between the Majin Buu Saga and Dragon Ball Super, and the story in Worlds Apart ends right after Super Hero, where the manga currently stops.
