Son Goku (born Kakarot, Age 737) is a Saiyan of , the younger son of and , sent from as a small child shortly before destroyed it. Found in the mountains of Earth by the hermit Gohan, a blow to the head erased the ruthless instincts of his race, and he grew up a cheerful, guileless boy with a monkey’s tail, a bottomless stomach and an untouchable gift for fighting.

From a hunt for the seven Dragon Balls at twelve, Goku became the greatest defender Earth has ever had: the first Super Saiyan in a thousand years, a fighter who has died twice for his friends and come back stronger both times, and eventually a mortal who could stand among gods and master Ultra Instinct, the technique of the Angels. He fights for the joy of it, but he has never fought for anything but others. The Archive splits his power across seven Champions; use the Form tabs in the infobox to see each one.

Each form is rated at the moment that defines it, not at Goku’s peak. Base Goku of the Saiyan Saga already sits at planetary level: stronger than anything on the Wizarding World’s scale by orders of magnitude, and in the same bracket as the Hulk at full fury or a Superman holding back. Every Super Saiyan grade raises the ceiling, and by Super Saiyan 2 and 3 he is in stellar territory, where only cosmic-tier Champions such as Thor at his strongest can trade blows with him.

With godly ki (Super Saiyan God and Blue) and Ultra Instinct he reaches universal tier: a clash between him and Beerus threatened the universe itself. What he never gains is reality-bending hax. He has no magic, no time manipulation and no mind powers beyond sensing ki, so Champions who rewrite rules (Doctor Strange, Scarlet Witch) remain genuinely dangerous to him. Ultra Instinct’s 10 in Combat reflects a technique that only the Angels and the gods of destruction have mastered.

History

Worlds Apart

Origins (Age 737)

The Saiyans were a warrior race who served the galactic tyrant as planet brokers, clearing worlds of their inhabitants so they could be sold. Kakarot was born on Planet Vegeta to Bardock, a low-class soldier, and Gine, a gentle Saiyan who had left the front lines to work as a butcher. His elder brother, , was already off on missions. At birth Kakarot’s power level was too low for a frontline posting, and the Saiyans’ custom was to send such infants alone to planets whose people were weak enough for a child to wipe out.

When Frieza abruptly recalled every Saiyan to Planet Vegeta, Bardock suspected a trap. He and Gine stole a space pod and launched their son towards a distant, little-known world called Earth, officially as an infant conqueror, in truth to get him out of Frieza’s reach. Bardock was right. Days later Frieza obliterated the planet and nearly every Saiyan on it, Kakarot’s parents among them. Only a handful of Saiyans off-world survived: , the prince, his bodyguard Nappa, Raditz, and the boy hurtling towards Earth.

Mount Paozu (Age 737–749)

The pod came down in the mountains of Mount Paozu, where an old martial artist, Gohan, found a squalling baby with a tail. The boy was violent and impossible to handle until he fell into a ravine and struck his head. After that he was a different child: sweet-natured, trusting, and happy. Gohan named him Goku, raised him as his grandson, and taught him the martial arts. As a final gift he left Goku the Four-Star Dragon Ball, which Goku kept as a keepsake of his grandfather, and an extending staff, the Power Pole.

Gohan warned him never to look at the full moon. One night Goku did, and the Saiyan within him took over: he became a Great Ape, a giant rampaging beast, and crushed his grandfather to death. Goku never remembered it. He believed a monster had killed Grandpa Gohan, and lived alone in the mountains afterwards, hunting fish and wild animals. He had never seen a city, and had never met a girl.

The search for the Dragon Balls (Age 749)

Main article:

At twelve, Goku met , a brilliant and bad-tempered teenage inventor who was hunting the seven Dragon Balls to wish for a perfect boyfriend. She noticed the Four-Star Ball he kept by his grandfather’s shrine and talked him into joining her. On the road they gathered a strange company: Oolong, a shapeshifting pig; Yamcha, a desert bandit too shy to talk to girls, and his partner Puar; and an old hermit, , who gave Goku the Flying Nimbus, a cloud only the pure of heart can ride, in thanks for saving his sea turtle. At Fire Mountain, Goku watched Roshi put out a raging blaze with a single beam of ki, the Kamehameha, and copied it on the spot.

At the end of the hunt, the small-time villain Emperor Pilaf gathered the balls first. Oolong stole the wish out from under him by asking for a pair of panties. Pilaf imprisoned the group, and under the full moon Goku transformed into a Great Ape again, smashing the castle open before Yamcha cut off his tail.

The Turtle School and the 21st Tournament (Age 749)

Goku went back to Roshi to train, alongside a bald young monk named who started as a rival and became the best friend he would ever have. For eight months Roshi worked them like farmhands: delivering milk, ploughing fields with their bare hands and swimming with sharks, all with heavy turtle shells on their backs. At the 21st World Martial Arts Tournament they burst onto the stage of the world’s greatest fighters. Goku reached the final and lost narrowly to Jackie Chun, who was Roshi in disguise, entered to keep his students humble. Roshi destroyed the moon during the match to stop Goku’s Great Ape form, and Goku’s tail grew back again soon after.

The Red Ribbon Army (Age 749–750)

Setting out to find his grandfather’s Four-Star Ball, Goku ran headlong into the Red Ribbon Army, a private military bent on gathering the Dragon Balls to conquer the world. Nobody had warned him that it was supposed to be impossible for one child to defeat an army. He scaled Muscle Tower in the snow, fought General Blue across a pirate cave, and was crushed by the assassin Mercenary Tao, the first opponent who nearly killed him. To beat Tao he climbed Korin’s Tower, chasing the Sacred Water, and gained something more useful: a sense of how to make his body faster and his blows sharper. He returned, defeated Tao, and stormed the Red Ribbon headquarters alone. At Fortuneteller Baba’s palace he faced her champion, a masked fighter who turned out to be Grandpa Gohan, briefly returned from the dead, and had one last joyful day with him.

Tien and King Piccolo (Age 753)

After three years of wandering and training, Goku returned for the 22nd Tournament. There he met and Chiaotzu, students of Roshi’s rival school, and lost the final to Tien when he struck a truck outside the ring a split second before Tien landed. Tien turned from a cold assassin into a lifelong friend during the match.

That night Krillin was murdered by Tambourine, a servant of the reborn demon . Goku hunted Piccolo down, lost, and nearly died. He climbed past Korin to drink the Ultra Divine Water, a poison that kills anyone unworthy of the power it awakens, survived it, and punched straight through King Piccolo’s chest. Before he died, the demon spat out an egg: his son and reincarnation, .

Kami’s Lookout and the 23rd Tournament (Age 753–756)

Goku spent three years training on the Lookout under Kami, the guardian of Earth and the good half of King Piccolo, and his attendant Mr. Popo, who taught him to fight without a wasted motion. Kami also removed his tail for good. At eighteen he returned to the 23rd Tournament unrecognisable. There he met Chi-Chi again, the daughter of the Ox-King whom he had rescued years before. As a boy he had promised to marry her without realising marriage was not a kind of food. He kept his word, agreeing to it in the middle of the ring. He then won the tournament, defeating Piccolo in a final that nearly levelled the stadium, and spared his life, because Kami and Piccolo shared one.

Family life (Age 756–761)

Goku and Chi-Chi settled on Mount Paozu. In Age 757 their son was born, and Goku named him after his grandfather. Chi-Chi, who wanted her son to be a scholar rather than a brawler, ran the house; Goku fished, farmed, and trained. They were, for a little while, entirely ordinary.

Raditz and the Saiyans (Age 761–762)

Main article:

At a reunion at Kame House, an armoured stranger landed on Roshi’s island and told Goku the truth: he was Kakarot, a Saiyan, and he had been sent to wipe out Earth. The stranger was his brother Raditz. When Goku refused to join him, Raditz kidnapped Gohan. Goku formed an uneasy alliance with Piccolo, and when even the two of them together could not beat Raditz, Goku locked his brother in a full nelson so that Piccolo could kill them both with the Special Beam Cannon.

In Other World, Kami arranged for Goku to train under King Kai, the North Kai, at the far end of Snake Way. Training under ten times Earth’s gravity on King Kai’s tiny planet, Goku learned the Kaioken and the Spirit Bomb. The Dragon Balls brought him back a year later, too late to save Yamcha, Tien, Chiaotzu or Piccolo from Vegeta and Nappa. He crushed Nappa without effort, then fought Vegeta himself: a brutal duel of Kaioken against the prince’s Galick Gun, and then against Vegeta as a Great Ape. Gohan, Krillin and Yajirobe finished it together. With Vegeta beaten and helpless, Goku begged Krillin to let him go. He had found a rival.

Namek and Frieza (Age 762)

Main article:

The Dragon Balls had died with Piccolo and Kami, so Gohan, Krillin and Bulma flew to , the Namekians’ homeworld, to find its own Dragon Balls. Goku followed in a ship of his own, training at a hundred times Earth’s gravity on the way until his body broke and healed again and again. He arrived to find Frieza’s elite Ginyu Force tearing his friends apart and broke the squad, even surviving Captain Ginyu stealing his body.

Frieza was another matter. He killed Vegeta before Goku’s eyes, and Vegeta, dying, told him the truth about the Saiyans and begged him to avenge them. Goku buried the prince and fought on, through a Kaioken pushed to twenty times and a Spirit Bomb drawn from Namek and the surrounding planets, and neither was enough. Then Frieza destroyed Krillin in front of him. Goku became the first Super Saiyan in a thousand years: golden-haired, teal-eyed, and for the first time in his life truly angry. He overpowered Frieza while the planet collapsed around them, offered him mercy, gave him energy when the tyrant was cut in half by his own attack, and only struck him down when Frieza used it to attack him again. Goku escaped the exploding planet in one of the Ginyu Force’s pods and landed on Yardrat, where he learned Instant Transmission.

The Androids and Cell (Age 764–767)

Main article:

When he came home, a young Super Saiyan from the future was already waiting for him. Future Trunks, Vegeta and Bulma’s son, had killed Frieza and his father, King Cold, and brought a warning: in three years, androids built by the Red Ribbon Army’s Dr. Gero would kill everyone, and Goku would die before them of a heart virus. He gave Goku the cure. When the androids came, the virus struck mid-fight, as promised, and Goku spent the first battles in bed.

Recovered, Goku took Gohan into the Hyperbolic Time Chamber and found a new path: rather than chasing higher grades of Super Saiyan, he mastered the form so completely that he could stay in it at rest, without strain. At the Cell Games he fought the perfect bio-android Cell to a standstill, then forfeited and named his eleven-year-old son as Earth’s champion. Gohan became a Super Saiyan 2 and surpassed them all. When Cell tried to destroy Earth by self-destructing, Goku used Instant Transmission to take him to King Kai’s planet, dying with him a second time. Cell survived, and Gohan killed him. Goku chose to stay dead, believing that the Earth’s enemies came for him. Months later, Chi-Chi gave birth to their second son, .

Seven years in Other World (Age 767–774)

Allowed to keep his body after death for his heroism, Goku spent seven years training in Other World among the great fighters of the past. There, out of reach of anyone on Earth, he achieved the second grade of Super Saiyan and then a third, whose power came with a body-draining cost and a mane of hair down to his waist.

Majin Buu (Age 774)

Main article:

Fortuneteller Baba gave Goku a single day on Earth to attend the 25th Tournament, where he met Goten for the first time. The day went wrong. The wizard Babidi was reviving the ancient monster Majin Buu, and he took control of Vegeta, who let himself be possessed in order to force a rematch with Goku. Their fight released enough energy to complete Buu’s revival. Vegeta later died trying to destroy Buu alone. Goku, his day nearly gone, unveiled Super Saiyan 3 to buy time and taught Goten and Trunks the Fusion Dance before returning to Other World.

The Old Kai gave his own life to restore Goku’s so that he could return and fight again. Goku fused with Vegeta through the Potara earrings into Vegito, who outclassed Super Buu and then let himself be absorbed to rescue their friends from inside him. When the original, Kid Buu, emerged and destroyed the Earth, Goku and Vegeta fought him on the Sacred World of the Kais. With the Earth wished back by the Namekian Dragon Balls, Vegeta called on everyone on Earth to lend their energy to Goku’s Spirit Bomb, and Mr. Satan talked the reluctant public into it; and Goku destroyed Kid Buu with it, asking that he be reborn one day as a good person so that they could fight again.

Daima (after Age 774)

Not long after the Buu crisis, , the new Supreme Demon King of the Demon Realm, came to Earth with his aide Degesu and , an elderly Namekian who had created the Demon Realm’s own Dragon Balls. Gomah used Earth’s Dragon Balls to wish everyone who had fought Majin Buu into children. Goku, Vegeta and the crossed into the Demon Realm to undo the wish, guided by a mysterious young man named and joined by a demon girl, Panzy.

The Demon Realm’s Dragon Balls were guarded by the Tamagami, powerful guardians Neva had created, and Goku had to win his battles in a child’s body. Gomah, meanwhile, seized the Evil Third Eye, which made him effectively unkillable. Goku revealed that he had been quietly developing a new form since the fight with Buu, and became a Super Saiyan 4: red-furred and wild-looking, but calmer and stronger than in any of his earlier grades. After Glorio’s wish restored the group to their adult bodies, adult Super Saiyan 4 Goku overwhelmed Gomah, though only ’s intervention, prying the Third Eye loose, made him beatable. He lost, but he held his own long enough that Beerus, delighted, spared the Earth. Goku and Vegeta began training under Beerus’s attendant and teacher, the Angel .

Physical appearance

Goku is a muscular man of average height with black eyes and spiked black hair that has kept exactly the same shape since he was a baby, as all Saiyan hair does. Saiyans age slowly and stay in their prime for decades, so in his forties he looks much as he did at twenty-five. As a child he had a brown monkey tail, which Kami removed for good. He almost always wears an orange martial arts gi over a blue undershirt with blue wristbands, a blue sash and boots, with the kanji of his current teacher on his chest and back: Roshi’s “Turtle”, King Kai’s symbol, and later his own “Go”. In the Super era he wears Whis’s symbol, and on Galactic Patrol missions a version of the Patrol’s uniform.

Each form changes him visibly. The Kaioken wraps him in a red aura. As a Super Saiyan his hair turns gold and stands up, and his eyes turn teal; the second grade sharpens the hair and adds crackling lightning; the third grows it to his waist and erases his eyebrows. Super Saiyan 4 brings red fur over his upper body, golden eyes ringed in red and wild black hair. Super Saiyan God makes him leaner, with red hair and eyes; Super Saiyan Blue, blue hair and eyes; and Ultra Instinct turns his eyes silver, then his hair.

Personality and traits

Goku is cheerful, simple and kind to a degree that baffles both friends and enemies. He takes people as they are, holds no grudges, and has almost no interest in money, status or anything he cannot eat or fight. He is not stupid, but he is profoundly uninterested in anything outside his own passions. He is naive about most of the world and astonishingly perceptive inside a fight, where he reads opponents, adapts mid-battle, and invents techniques on the spot.

His defining trait is his love of fighting. A strong opponent excites him in a way nothing else does, and this has repeatedly put others in danger. He let Vegeta go, gave Frieza energy, forfeited to Hit, and nudged Zeno towards the Tournament of Power, each time because he wanted to see how strong someone could become. His friends, Chi-Chi most of all, have long resented that he can be a careless father and husband. Yet his love of battle is never cruel. He fights to test himself, not to dominate, and every fight ends with an offer of friendship to anyone who will take it. Piccolo, Vegeta, Tien, Majin Buu and even Beerus became allies because Goku would not stop offering.

Goku is slow to anger and almost impossible to hate. When he truly is angry, it is because someone has hurt the people he loves. That anger made him a Super Saiyan. He is self-sacrificing to the point of recklessness: he has died for others twice and accepted a third death in the Cell Games without hesitation. He is also humble about it, quick to credit others and genuinely happy when his rivals, or his sons, surpass him.

Abilities and techniques

Saiyan physiology

As a Saiyan, Goku has immense natural strength and an appetite to match. Saiyans grow dramatically stronger whenever they recover from near-fatal injuries, and his training has often deliberately exploited this. As a child he could become a Great Ape under the full moon, multiplying his power tenfold at the cost of his reason.

Martial arts

Goku is one of the greatest martial artists in his universe, trained by Grandpa Gohan, Master Roshi, Korin, Kami, King Kai, Whis and Merus. He fights mostly unarmed, with a fluid, improvisational style that absorbs whatever he sees. He copied the Kamehameha by watching it once, and later picked up the Solar Flare and others the same way. He reads opponents unusually well and often wins by learning from earlier defeats.

Ki

Goku can fly, fire energy blasts, sense other people’s ki over vast distances, and conceal his own. Godly ki, which he gained as a Super Saiyan God, cannot be sensed by mortals at all.

Kamehameha

Master Roshi’s signature technique, a wave of ki gathered in cupped hands. It is Goku’s signature move, and he has fired it with his feet, redirected it in mid-air, and used it to kill the resurrected Frieza.

Kaioken

King Kai’s technique, which multiplies everything about a fighter at once (strength, speed, ki) in a red aura, at the cost of shredding the user’s body when pushed too far. Goku used it up to twenty times against Frieza. Later in life he layered it over Super Saiyan Blue.

Spirit Bomb

A sphere gathered from energy lent by every living thing nearby, from plants to planets. Only someone with a pure heart can wield it; to anyone evil it is lethal, but it passes harmlessly through the good. Goku used it to defeat Kid Buu with the help of everyone on Earth.

Instant Transmission

Learned on Yardrat, it lets Goku teleport instantly to anyone whose ki he can sense, anywhere in the universe, and to the other world.

Fusion

With Vegeta, Goku has fused into Vegito through the Potara earrings and into Gogeta through the Fusion Dance. Both fusions are far stronger than either Saiyan alone.

Ultra Instinct

The technique of the Angels and the gods of destruction: the body moves and reacts by itself, without the delay of thought. Show this form

Possessions

  • Power Pole: an extending staff given to him by Grandpa Gohan. He fought with it throughout his childhood.
  • Flying Nimbus: Master Roshi’s gift, a cloud only the pure of heart can ride. Goku has ridden it all his life.
  • Four-Star Dragon Ball: his grandfather’s keepsake, and the reason he joined Bulma.
  • Senzu beans: magic beans grown by Korin that fully heal a fighter and fill his stomach for ten days.
  • Weighted clothing: the heavy wristbands, boots and gi he wears to train.
  • Zeno’s button: a device for summoning the Omni-King, which he used to call Future Zeno.

Relationships

Family

She runs his life and their house, and she has never stopped resenting the fights that keep taking him away, even as she loves him. , the son he named after his grandfather, has the greatest potential in the family and wants to be a scholar; Goku trusts him completely and is proudest of him when Gohan surpasses him. was born after Goku’s death and was seven before they met. He adores his father and is the most like him. Goku’s granddaughter takes after him too.

Goku never knew his parents. and sent him away to save him and died with their planet. Only late in life did he learn something of his father, when Granolah told him how Bardock had spared him as a child. His brother was the first enemy he died fighting. raised him, and Goku grieved him for years without knowing what had really happened.

Vegeta

Goku’s rival for life. The Saiyan prince arrived as an enemy and despised the low-class warrior who kept outclassing him, but his rivalry with Goku slowly became the centre of his life. For Goku, Vegeta is the opponent who has pushed him further than anyone, and through Vegito and Gogeta the two have literally become one. He accepts Vegeta’s pride and never takes it personally.

Krillin

His best friend since they trained together under Roshi as children. Krillin’s first two deaths, at Tambourine’s hands and then Frieza’s, drove Goku to kill King Piccolo and to become a Super Saiyan. Goku has always seen in Krillin the proof that courage matters more than power.

Bulma

The first friend he made outside the mountains. Bulma introduced him to the world and has been the brains behind most of his adventures since, building his ships, his gravity chambers and his radar. She treats him like an exasperating younger brother.

Piccolo

Once his sworn enemy, the reincarnation of King Piccolo became Goku’s ally against Raditz and then Gohan’s mentor. Goku trusts him with his son’s training, and later with Pan.

Master Roshi

Goku’s first true teacher. Behind the old man’s lechery Goku found the foundation of everything he does, and he still treats Roshi with real respect.

Frieza

The tyrant who destroyed his people and killed Krillin, and the one opponent Goku has truly hated. Even so, Goku spared him on Namek and later recruited him for the Tournament of Power, where they fought Jiren side by side. Frieza’s hatred of Goku has only grown.

Beerus and Whis

The God of Destruction he challenged at their first meeting, and the Angel who became his teacher in the Super era. Goku treats both with the same easy friendliness he gives anyone, which alarms everyone else around him.

Etymology

Son Goku is the Japanese reading of Sun Wukong, the Monkey King of the Chinese classic Journey to the West, on which the early Dragon Ball is loosely based; the monkey tail, the extending staff and the flying cloud all come from that story. The characters of Goku mean “awakened to emptiness”. His Saiyan name, Kakarot, is a pun on “carrot”: Saiyan names are puns on vegetables, from Vegeta to Raditz (radish) and Bardock (burdock).

Trivia

  • Goku has died twice in his own timeline (against Raditz and against Cell) and been brought back both times. In Future Trunks’s timeline he died of the heart virus.
  • He was the first Super Saiyan in a thousand years and, through the ritual, the first Super Saiyan God of his era.
  • His Champion forms span every major era of his life, from the Saiyan Saga (Base) to the Tournament of Power (Ultra Instinct -Sign-).
  • Both of his Champion themes come from Dragon Ball Z Dokkan Battle.
  • He named his eldest son after the grandfather who raised him.
