Chao Zheng (鄭潮, born 1979) is a witch who was sorted into in 1990, a year above , and played Seeker for her house. In Harry’s fourth year she was the girl he could barely speak around. She turned down his invitation to the Yule Ball kindly, because she was already going with , and she was the person Cedric brought up out of the lake in the second task., “The Invitation”, “The Second Task”

Cedric’s murder in 1995 left her grieving for most of the next year. She joined all the same, and grew close to Harry there, though the two of them ended as friends. In the spring of 1996 forced a truth potion on her and learned everything about the Army; Harry refused to let anyone blame her for it, and she came back to them., “Exposed” Having left school in 1997, she returned through the Hog’s Head to fight in the Battle of Hogwarts in 1998, and survived it.

Biography

Early life

Chao Zheng was born in Britain in 1979, to a family of Chinese descent. Little else is recorded of her family or her childhood. She began at in September 1990 and was sorted into Ravenclaw.

Ravenclaw Seeker (1993–1994)

By her fourth year Chao was the Seeker for the Ravenclaw house team, flying a Comet Two Sixty. Ravenclaw met Gryffindor that winter, soon after Harry got his Firebolt back, and Harry, who had never paid much attention to her before, found her hard to stop watching and the Snitch hard to concentrate on. Late in the match he turned on three hooded figures at the edge of the pitch with his first full Patronus. They were and her friends in black robes, trying to frighten him off his broom. Ravenclaw lost the match., “Quidditch”

The Triwizard year (1994–1995)

The Triwizard Tournament came to Hogwarts in Chao’s fifth year, and the Goblet of Fire named Cedric Diggory, the Hufflepuff captain and Seeker, as the school’s champion. By the winter he and Chao were together. Harry spent weeks working up the nerve to ask her to the Yule Ball. When he finally did, she said no as gently as she could and told him the truth: she was already going with Cedric. At the ball she danced with Cedric., “The Yule Ball”

For the second task each champion had an hour in the black lake to recover the person they would miss most, and the judges chose the people the whole school could see they cared for. Chao was Cedric’s. He brought her up safely.

In June, Cedric took the Triwizard Cup with Harry and was murdered by Lord Voldemort in a graveyard. At the leaving feast Dumbledore asked the school to remember him, and the Hufflepuff table rose first. Chao could not stand at all., “The Leaving Feast”

Dumbledore’s Army (1995–1996)

With the Ministry denying that Voldemort had returned, and Umbridge teaching Defence Against the Dark Arts from a book, Harry’s friends pushed him into teaching it himself. Chao was among the twenty-odd students from three houses who signed their names in the back room of the Hog’s Head, and she trained with the Army in the Room of Requirement all year., “Dumbledore’s Army” She had more reason than most of them to believe what was coming.

She and Harry grew close in the Army, and on Valentine’s Day they went to Hogsmeade together. It didn’t work. She kept coming back to Cedric, whom she hadn’t finished grieving, and Harry kept losing the thread to someone across the street. Each saw that the other was somewhere else, and it ended without a quarrel. They stayed friends, which suited them both better., “Valentine’s Day”

That spring Umbridge learned that something was going on in the castle. She had Chao dragged into her office and poured Veritaserum down her throat, a potion the Ministry’s own rules allowed only under strict control. Chao told her everything without wanting to say any of it. The Army was exposed; Dumbledore took the blame on himself and escaped the castle rather than be arrested, and Umbridge became headmistress.

Harry refused to blame her, and said so loudly, to anyone who would listen. Chao came back to the Army the day she could stand to, and it met in secret again before the month was out.

Later years

Chao finished her seventh year in 1997 and left Hogwarts, the year Dumbledore died.

On the night of the Battle of Hogwarts in May 1998, word reached the Army’s former members, and those who had already left school came through the Hog’s Head and up the passage into the Room of Requirement. Chao was one of them. When the houses gave their answer to Voldemort, she stood on the walls with the Ravenclaws, under ’s lead, and she survived the battle., “The Return to Hogwarts” What she did after the war is not recorded.

Physical appearance

Chao has long, black hair. She was a pretty fourth-year when Harry first flew against her, and he found it hard to keep his mind on the Snitch.

Personality and traits

Chao is kind and honest, and she is braver than she looks. When Harry asked her to the ball she could have laughed or made an excuse; she told him the truth and was sorry for it. After Cedric’s death she grieved openly and for a long time, and she did not hide it to make anyone else comfortable. The grief was real and earned, and it was the honest reason the Valentine’s Day trip went nowhere.

She joined an illegal defence group in the same year she was mourning the boy Voldemort had killed, against a Ministry that said he had not been killed by anyone. After Umbridge took the Army’s secrets out of her by force, the easiest thing would have been to stay away. She came back.

Magical abilities and skills

  • Flying: Chao played Seeker for Ravenclaw from at least her fourth year, flying a Comet Two Sixty.
  • Defensive magic: She trained with Dumbledore’s Army through 1995 and 1996 and could cast a corporeal Patronus, a silver swan, which was advanced magic for a student.
  • Duelling: Two years after the Army’s last meeting she was able to hold the walls of Hogwarts against the Death Eaters, and to walk away from it.

Relationships

Cedric Diggory

Chao and Cedric were rival Seekers before they were anything else. They were together by the winter of the Triwizard Tournament, went to the Yule Ball as a couple, and in the second task she was the person he would miss most. His murder at the end of that year was the great loss of her time at school, and she carried it through the year that followed.

Harry Potter

Harry noticed Chao in his third year and spent his fourth working up the courage to ask her out. She turned him down kindly. In the Army they grew close, and on Valentine’s Day 1996 they found they were each thinking of someone else, and settled happily on friendship. When the Army was exposed through her, Harry was the first to say, and the loudest, that it was not her fault.

Hermione Granger

Chao and shared a house, a year apart. The Army’s exposure happened inside Ravenclaw, and in 1998 Chao fought on the walls among the Ravenclaws that Hermione led.

Dolores Umbridge

Umbridge forced a truth potion on a sixteen-year-old student to break up a group of children learning to defend themselves. It is part of the Ministry’s record in this continuity, and no one at the Ministry ever answered for it.

Etymology

Her name is written 鄭潮 in Chinese, and read with the surname first, Zhèng Cháo. Zheng (鄭) is a common Chinese surname, taken from the ancient state of Zheng in the Zhou dynasty. Chao (潮) means “tide.”

Changes from Canon

  • Cho Chang is renamed Chao Zheng (鄭潮).
  • She is Harry’s crush in his fourth year only. They never kiss or date; their Valentine’s Day in Hogsmeade ends with the two of them as friends.
  • Her grief for Cedric is treated with respect, not as a burden on Harry.
  • Marietta Edgecombe does not appear. The Army is exposed because Umbridge forces Veritaserum on Chao, and there is no jinx on the parchment to punish anyone.
  • Harry publicly refuses to blame her, and she returns to Dumbledore’s Army.

Trivia

  • Chao was the person Cedric would miss most in the second task, just as Ron was Harry’s.
  • Harry was still smarting from Chao’s refusal when found him and made her own sideways invitation, so the evening Chao said no is also the evening that led, by a long way round, to the Yule Ball as it happened.
  • The “someone across the street” Harry kept losing the thread to on Valentine’s Day 1996 was a girl in a green-and-silver scarf: Dracaena Malfoy.
  • More than a year later, arguing with Dracaena in a corridor, Harry threw it back at her that the first thing she had done after Chao turned him down was mock him for it.

Behind the scenes

  • In the original books the character is named Cho Chang, a name widely criticised as unconvincing for a Chinese girl, often read as two surnames put side by side. Chao Zheng is a name a Chinese family could give a daughter.
  • In the original Order of the Phoenix, Harry and Cho kiss and briefly date, and the Army is betrayed by her friend Marietta Edgecombe, whose face Hermione’s jinx scars for it. Here Harry’s fifth year belongs to Dracaena, and the betrayal is taken out of a student by force.
  • Chao is a minor character and not a playable Champion. Hers is the first Archive article for a character who is not a Champion.
