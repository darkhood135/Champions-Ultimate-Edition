The Winter Soldier

James Buchanan "Bucky" Barnes

Blaster

Captain America’s partner in the fires of World War II, stolen from death and remade into the Cold War’s most feared assassin, James Buchanan “Bucky” Barnes now fights to atone for the blood on his hands as the Winter Soldier.

History remembered Bucky Barnes as a casualty: the young soldier at Captain America’s side who fell from an exploding drone plane in the last days of the war and was swallowed by the icy sea. The truth was crueler. Soviet hands pulled him from the water with his left arm gone and his memory shattered, and they rebuilt him into a weapon. His mind was wiped and rewritten, and his body was kept frozen between missions. That way the ghost his handlers called the Winter Soldier could surface across half a century of assassinations and vanish again without aging a day. When Steve Rogers finally broke through the conditioning and gave him back his name, he gave him back everything else too: every face, every target, every life ended by hands that were his but never by his choice. Bucky does not ask to be forgiven for any of it, and some days he doesn’t believe he deserves to be. Instead he works, quietly and relentlessly, turning the skills his captors drilled into him toward the very people they once sent him to destroy. Beneath the guarded silence and clipped soldier’s speech is still the loyal kid who would have followed his best friend anywhere. With him is a stubborn hope he rarely voices: that one day he might be more than the worst things done in his name.

His captors made sure the weapon would last. A crude copy of the super-soldier formula pushed his strength, speed, stamina and reflexes far past any ordinary man’s, and decades of cryogenic sleep left him barely older than the day he fell. In place of the arm he lost is a cybernetic limb of staggering power. It is strong enough to crush steel and tear through armor, fast enough to catch a blow out of the air, and built to unleash concussive energy and crackling electromagnetic bursts that can fry any machine within reach. Behind it stands the finest training the Cold War could buy. He is a marksman who can make a shot from a mile out and an infiltrator who slips through locked doors and guarded halls like smoke. He has mastered the knife, the fist and nearly every weapon ever made, and he speaks the languages of the many nations he was sent into. He was built to be the perfect tool of someone else’s will. Every day since, he has chosen for himself where to aim it.

"People tell me... I've redeemed myself. And some days... I think that's true. Other days... I don't. Helping people... helps me. To feel... worthwhile again. And maybe, hopefully... it'll help make those days where I really do feel like I've turned a corner... not be so rare."

Till the End of the Line w/ Captain America

Steve Rogers is the one thread that runs unbroken through every version of Bucky Barnes: the kid who tagged along, the soldier who fought beside him, and the ghost sent to kill him. Even buried under decades of conditioning, something in the Winter Soldier recognized the man he’d been ordered to destroy. It was Steve’s refusal to raise a hand against him that finally cracked the ice. Bucky has never known what to do with faith like that. He’s carried Steve’s shield and watched his back across two lifetimes, and he still feels the debt sitting heavy in his chest. Steve calls it friendship and insists there’s nothing to repay. Bucky has decided to spend the rest of his life repaying it anyway.

Debts in Blood w/ Iron Man

Of all the faces the Winter Soldier left behind, Howard and Maria Stark’s are the ones Bucky can never stop seeing, because their son is standing right in front of him. He remembers the snowy road, the wreck and the order he carried out without a flicker of hesitation. He also remembers the look on Tony’s face the day he learned the truth. Bucky has never offered an excuse and never will; he knows no words could make that night right. So he does the only thing left to him. He fights at Tony’s side, takes the hits meant for him, and keeps his distance when the anger flares. He doesn’t expect forgiveness. He’s only grateful, quietly and constantly, that Tony keeps choosing to stand beside him anyway.

Men Out of Time w/ Wolverine

Logan is one of the very few people alive who remembers Bucky Barnes from before, as a cocky young soldier in a foxhole in 1944. He’s one of fewer still who crossed paths with what came after. They’re two old soldiers who have each lived far longer than any man should, carrying more blood and more names than they’ll ever say aloud, and neither has much patience for the other’s brooding. Logan doesn’t coddle him, doesn’t absolve him, and doesn’t flinch at the worst of what he’s done; he’s done plenty of the same. For Bucky that’s a rare kind of relief: a man who knows exactly what it costs to keep getting back up, and who simply expects him to.

Weapons Unmade w/ Batgirl

Cassandra Cain was raised in silence and learned to read the language of the body before she ever spoke a word. Her father built her from childhood into the perfect killer, just as Bucky’s handlers rebuilt him. She took a life once, as a child, and has refused every day since to take another. In her, Bucky sees the redemption he’s still chasing: someone made into a weapon who chose, against everything she was taught, to be something else. She doesn’t need words to read the guilt he carries, and he doesn’t need them to understand hers. Between the two of them the silence says enough, and side by side in a fight they move like two people who have finally unlearned the same lesson.

The Harm They Didn't Choose w/ Dracaena Malfoy

James Barnes recognized the look in Dracaena's eyes long before he knew her story, because he's worn it himself for the better part of a century — the particular hollowness of someone made to do terrible things by a will that wasn't their own, then left holding the guilt once the strings were finally cut. He knows the trap of it intimately: how easy it is to either excuse yourself completely or drown in a blame that was never wholly yours to carry. So he doesn't hand her easy absolution, and he doesn't let her wallow, either. What he offers is the quiet company of someone a little further down the same long road — living proof that you can carry what you were forced to do and still, slowly, become someone worth carrying it.

Changelog

Balance changes since this Champion's release. Newest first.

v1.1.0 October 22, 2026
  • Nerf Durability — MAX HP 8,600 → 8,200. The 2% HP restore on damage moved out of Red Star into Assassin’s Arsenal, so his sustain is now a passive choice instead of always on.
  • Rework Leader reworked — Blood On Clean Hands (“Firearm Expert” or “Road to Redemption” allies +15% ATK and MAG) → One Man Army (“Firearm Expert” or “Street Level” allies +12% ATK and MAG).
  • Nerf Tainted Voltage no longer restores EN (still restores 1 STA per enemy damaged). Also applies to Tracking Voltage.
  • Nerf Kraken Impact — EN restored per enemy slammed is now capped at 45 EN.
  • Nerf Mechanical Slam — damage 300% → 200% ATK twice; splash 200% → 160% ATK (target excluded).
  • Nerf Surging Strength — now also prevents EN gain for 1 turn, so an Overdrive turn can no longer be refuelled.
  • Nerf Vibranium Guard — the +8% ATK on attacks received while blocking now stacks up to 4 times in 3 turns.
  • Buff Sparking Malfunction — cooldown reduction 1 → 2 turns.
  • Rework Red Star — ULT gain clarified: 2% when one of his abilities damages at least one enemy, once per ability used.
  • Rework Hidden Blade — wording clarified: inflicts “Bleed” for 5 turns if the target does not have “Bleed”; otherwise inflicts “Deep Wound” for 1 turn.
  • Buff Synergy w/ Iron Man — Protective Redirection’s “Taunt” lasts 1 extra turn if Iron Man is within 3 panels.
  • Rework Synergy w/ Wolverine — Berserk Impact damage 380% → 350% ATK, EN restore 20 → 15 per enemy slammed (up to 45). New passive The Cost of Getting Back Up: endures a fatal blow while Wolverine is not defeated (once per battle, takes priority over Red Star).
  • Nerf Synergy w/ Dracaena Malfoy — Drowning in Guilt renamed Living Proof; “Road to Redemption” ally bonus +7% → +6% ATK and MAG.
