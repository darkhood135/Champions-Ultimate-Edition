The Summer After is an interlude in , between and . It covers the summer of 1998, when and left Hogwarts together and made a home of their own.

Story

On Their Own

Harry and Dracaena stepped off the Hogwarts Express with nowhere to go. Harry would not go back to his aunt and uncle, and Dracaena’s home had gone with the fortune she gave away. For the first weeks of the summer they lived in a hotel in London, learning, a little awkwardly, how to run a life with no one else in charge of it.

It was there that Harry learned number four, Privet Drive was for sale, at a price meant to move it quickly. The Dursleys had come home from hiding to a street that knew too much about them, and wanted to be gone. Dracaena told him they could live anywhere he liked. Harry decided to go and see it, if only to face the place one last time.

Number Four, Privet Drive

The Dursleys would not sell to him. Vernon and Petunia met them at the door in a rage, and Harry’s attempt at civility lasted only as long as their insults did. Dracaena kept silent until she could not, then turned on them herself. It was Dudley who ended it. He stood up for his cousin against his own parents, and kept standing until they gave way.

They let the two of them look around. The Dursleys would not show them the house, so Harry gave Dracaena the tour himself, ending at the cupboard under the stairs where he had slept for ten years. They bought the house. Hedwig came home to it from the Burrow, and took to Dracaena at once, far more warmly than she had ever taken to most people.

The Birthday

Harry turned eighteen on the last day of July. Dracaena planned a surprise party for him in secret, with Ron and Hermione helping in their very different ways. When Harry came home that evening the house was full: the Weasleys, Neville, Luna and more of his friends, in the house where his birthday had never once been celebrated. Before the night was out he asked Dracaena on a proper first date.

The First Date

Harry planned the date himself, down to the last detail. That night Harry and Dracaena became lovers.

Afterward, Harry found the scars his own spell had left across her two years before. He broke down and apologized, and she held him until he could stop.

They had a home, and each other. The reckoning with the Ministry was still waiting.
