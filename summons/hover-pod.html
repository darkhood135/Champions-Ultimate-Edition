Hover Pod

Frieza's Hover Pod

Booster

With a low hum of alien machinery, Frieza's personal hover pod glides onto the field — the floating throne from which an emperor surveys the ruin of worlds without ever deigning to stand. More command station than vehicle, its armored shell bristles with sensors that read a battlefield at a glance and systems that answer to its occupant's slightest whim, bearing them above the carnage with unbothered, gliding ease. It is a statement as much as a machine: that the one who rides it need not run, need not strain, need not so much as rise from their seat to reign over all beneath them. To sit in that pod is to regard a battle as a thing already won — and to remind every soul below exactly how far above them the master of the Frieza Force holds himself to be.

Passive Abilities
Mobile Throne Passive 1
  • The summoner can ride and dismount from this summon at will.
  • The rider cannot perform melee attacks while riding.
  • The rider of this summon, when moving, will consume the summon's STA first (until depleted).
  • The rider of this summon is considered airborne but cannot be grounded.
Protective Shell Passive 2
  • 70% of the damage directed at the rider will instead be directed at this summon.
  • This summon restores 5 STA per turn.
  • Cannot receive healing or buffs from any source.
  • Expires after 3 turns.
