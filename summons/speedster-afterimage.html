Speedster Afterimage

Remnant of the Past

Breaker

Run fast enough, and the world can no longer keep up. For a speedster bound to the Speed Force, there are moments when the body races so far ahead of the present that the instant it left behind refuses to let go, and an afterimage of the runner stands where they were a heartbeat ago, crackling with lightning, still caught mid-stride. Most speedsters leave them as nothing more than a blur on the air, gone the moment they’re seen. The Flash learned to give them weight. Doubling back through his own last few seconds, he pins a version of himself out of the immediate past onto the present: a remnant of who he was a moment ago, wearing the same face and crackling with the same lightning. It throws no punches of its own. It is no clone and no true illusion, only time running a step behind, and from across a battlefield there is no telling it from the man himself. Strike it and the light it is made of bursts in the attacker’s eyes; ignore it and it holds its ground, drawing fire meant for the real speedster, who is already somewhere else. Two Flashes are a problem; a Flash who might be standing in any of the places he just was is a far worse one. But the Speed Force will only hold a moment out of place for so long, and when the afterimage is struck down or its time runs out, it does not fall. It simply catches up, collapsing into a streak of lightning that races home to the runner who left it behind, as though it had only been waiting to be let go.

"Keep up!"
