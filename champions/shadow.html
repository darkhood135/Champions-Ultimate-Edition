Shadow the Hedgehog

Blaster

Engineered fifty years ago aboard the Space Colony ARK as the Ultimate Life Form, Shadow the Hedgehog wields the power of chaos in service of a single promise — Sonic’s dark rival, and the most dangerous being ever made in the name of saving a life.

Shadow was created to be perfect. Professor Gerald Robotnik, the greatest scientific mind of his age, built him in the depths of the ARK as the Ultimate Life Form — ageless, deathless, and impossibly powerful — in hopes of curing his granddaughter Maria’s fatal illness. Maria became Shadow’s only friend, the one person who ever showed him a life beyond the purpose he was made for. Then soldiers stormed the colony to shut the project down, and she was shot while sending him to safety, with one last wish on her lips: that he give the people of Earth a chance to be happy. Grief broke the Professor, who twisted Shadow’s memories toward vengeance, and when Shadow awoke half a century later, he came terrifyingly close to destroying the world Maria had loved. Only at the very end did he remember her true words, and he gave everything to save it instead. He has lived by that promise ever since. Cold, proud, and fiercely alone, Shadow trusts almost no one and explains himself to no one. He serves alongside G.U.N. not out of loyalty but because it lets him keep his word, and he would sooner fight Sonic than thank him. He would never call himself a hero. Still, no one who has seen him fight to keep that promise could call him anything else.

Shadow matches Sonic stride for stride, skating across the ground on rocket-powered air shoes, but his true strength is drawn from chaos itself. With a single Chaos Emerald in hand he can invoke Chaos Control, bending time and space to vanish in an instant or still the world around him; he hurls Chaos Spears, bolts of raw energy that strike from afar, and when pushed to the edge he unleashes a Chaos Blast that obliterates everything near him. Even that is power held in check. The golden inhibitor rings on his wrists and ankles exist only to restrain him, and he takes them off only when he means to finish the fight. Gather all seven Emeralds, and he becomes Super Shadow, a blazing golden avatar of everything he was built to be. Shadow is a living weapon by design, the deadliest thing the Professor ever made. But he has decided for himself what that weapon is for: keeping a promise to a girl who believed the world deserved saving.

"I'm letting go of that destructive anger. But I will continue to fight. Fight... with your wish in my heart. Your wish for everyone to have a chance to be happy."

Faker w/ Sonic

Sonic called him a faker the day they met, and Shadow shot back that Sonic wasn’t even good enough to be his fake. Neither has ever taken it back. Sonic runs for the fun of it; Shadow runs because there is always something that must be done, and nothing about the other’s carefree grin has ever sat right with him. Yet Sonic is the only one alive who has matched him stride for stride, the only rival Shadow has never once been able to dismiss. More than once they have stood together in gold to save a world, and in those moments the rivalry falls away into something Shadow will never call trust out loud. He’ll keep testing himself against Sonic for as long as they both draw breath. And though he will never say it, there is no one he would rather have running beside him when everything is on the line.
