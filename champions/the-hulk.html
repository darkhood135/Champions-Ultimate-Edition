The Hulk

Robert Bruce Banner

Defender

A brilliant nuclear physicist caught in the blast of his own gamma bomb, Dr. Bruce Banner now shares his body with the Hulk — a towering green giant of limitless rage, and the strongest one there is.

Bruce Banner has spent most of his life running from a monster, and the hardest truth he has ever faced is that the monster was always a part of him. Long before the gamma bomb, he was a frightened boy in a house ruled by his father’s fists. He learned early that every tear and every flash of anger could set off something worse, so he buried them all as deep as they would go. He grew into a quiet, guarded man and one of the finest scientific minds of his generation, until the day a teenager wandered onto the testing range of the bomb he had built. Banner ran onto the field, threw the boy into a trench, and took the full force of the blast himself. That night, everything he had locked away came roaring out. The world has hunted the Hulk ever since: the army that once employed Banner, a public that calls him a menace, even the heroes he stood beside as a founding Avenger before he walked away. Only among the misfits of the Defenders has he found anything close to a place to belong. Through it all, the Hulk wants little more than what he has never been given, which is to be left alone. Yet again and again he finds himself standing between the innocent and something worse than himself. The uneasy truce between the man and the monster has slowly grown into something deeper: two halves of one soul learning that neither can survive without the other.

The Hulk is a force of nature. Gamma radiation remakes Banner’s slight frame into a mountain of green muscle, and his strength has no known ceiling. The madder Hulk gets, the stronger Hulk gets, his power climbing with every surge of fury until he is shattering mountains and shrugging off blows that would level cities. A single bound carries him for miles, and a clap of his hands sends out a shockwave that flattens anything in its path. His gamma-charged body heals from nearly any wound in moments, endures the crushing deep of the ocean and the void of space, and refuses, time and again, to stay down. Behind it all is Banner’s own genius, among the greatest on Earth in the very radiation that made him. He has spent years trying to cure the Hulk, and longer still learning to understand him. The rage that first broke free to shield a frightened little boy was never only a curse. It was a protector all along, and it has never stopped standing guard over those who cannot protect themselves.

Storm and Fury w/ Thor

Most of the world sees the Hulk and runs. Thor sees him and grins. They have clashed since the earliest days of the Avengers, the God of Thunder against the strongest one there is, in battles that leveled mountains and settled nothing, and the question of which of them is truly stronger has never been answered. For the Hulk, that is half the appeal. Thor is one of the precious few who can take everything he has and keep coming, and when the dust clears, the Asgardian claps him on the shoulder like a brother instead of chaining him like a beast. Hulk would never say it aloud, and he would deny it if asked, but there is something close to joy in a fight he doesn’t have to win, against someone who has always treated him as a warrior rather than a monster.

Genius and Betrayal w/ Iron Man

Banner and Stark were among the first to answer the call that formed the Avengers, and in each other they found something rare: a mind that could keep pace. Bruce admires Tony’s brilliance and drive, and Tony is one of the few who has ever seen the Hulk as a problem worth solving instead of a monster worth killing. But Tony solves problems his own way. He built the Hulkbuster armor for the day his friend lost control. When he and his secret council decided the Hulk was too dangerous for Earth, they tricked him onto a ship and launched him into the stars. The Hulk came home with an army and a grudge, and Iron Man was first on his list. Their friendship has never fully healed. They still fight side by side, two geniuses who respect each other completely and trust each other only so far.

Beasts Within w/ Wolverine

The first time Logan came for him, he was an agent of the Canadian government, sent into the northern wilds to bring the Hulk down. He has been coming back ever since. Their fights are legend: claws that can cut through anything against skin that almost nothing can cut, two bodies that heal faster than either can break the other, and neither one with the sense to quit. The Hulk calls him a pest and means it. But Banner understands Logan better than almost anyone. Logan spends every day caging the animal inside him, and Banner has spent his whole life losing that same fight. Above all, the Hulk respects the little man for one thing: no matter how far he gets thrown, Logan always gets back up, and he always looks the Hulk in the eye when he does.

Born of Rage w/ Goku (Super Saiyan)

In Goku, the Hulk met someone he could not make sense of: a man who smiles through every battle and asks strong opponents for a fight the way other people offer a handshake. He has asked the Hulk more times than anyone can count. Hulk keeps telling him no. Hulk does not want to fight. Yet Banner sees something familiar beneath the golden hair. The Super Saiyan was born the day Goku watched his best friend die and his grief turned to fury. It was the same kind of rage that first broke free to shield a frightened boy from a world he couldn’t survive alone. The difference is that Goku learned to carry that fury and set it down again at will, a peace Banner has chased for years. Every so often the Hulk gives in to a sparring match. It may be the only fight of his life where he gets to let go completely and nobody gets hurt.

Pawn of Mischief w/ Loki

The Avengers exist because Loki tried to use the Hulk. Hungry for revenge on his brother, the God of Mischief cast an illusion of the Hulk wrecking a railway, hoping to draw Thor into a battle that would finish him. Instead, the heroes who came running banded together as Earth’s Mightiest Heroes, with the Hulk among them. The Hulk has never forgotten being made a puppet. The whole world already treats him like a weapon to be aimed, and Loki is the one who actually tried to pull the trigger. The trickster still sees him as a blunt instrument, too simple to see through a lie. That is Loki’s mistake. The Hulk cares nothing for schemes or clever words, which makes him the one problem the liar can’t talk his way out of. And when Loki’s mouth runs too long, the Hulk has a very simple answer.

Changelog

Balance changes since this Champion's release. Newest first.

v1.1.0 October 23, 2026
  • Rework Empowered by Anger reworked — no longer starts at 70% damage reduction that drops by 5% per “Mounting Rage” stack. Now gains 3% damage reduction per stack (up to 45%), so the madder Hulk gets, the tougher he gets. “Mounting Rage” is now gained once per ability received, so multi-hit attacks no longer stack it per hit.
  • Buff Empowered by Anger — ULT gain 1% → 2% per attack received, now once per ability. “Cannot be pushed or launched by enemies” is now “Cannot be moved by enemy effects (except portals or teleportation)”.
  • Buff Monstrous Leap — now targets a 2x2 landing area, ignores elevation and inflicts “Bruise” for 2 turns to all enemies hit. Also applies to Earth-Destroying Slam.
  • Rework Thunder Clap — now hits all enemies in a 2-wide line (range 10) instead of one target. Damage 200% → 300% MAG. EN 20 → 30. “Paralyze” is guaranteed only on the first enemy hit; every other enemy has a 30% chance.
  • Rework Confronting Roar — at 5 or more stacks it no longer restores 12% HP. Instead it restores 50 EN (was 30) and 5 STA and grants “Might” for 2 turns.
  • Rework Gamma Surge — no longer reduces damage by 12% for 3 attacks or grants “Might”. Now grants +30% DEF and RES for 2 turns and restores 20% HP.
  • Rework Power Throw — direction is now chosen (was random). The target is thrown over everything in between and lands 6 panels away. Landing on an obstacle or enemy deals 300% ATK to both. Landing on a void panel deals 400% ATK and the target falls. An empty panel still deals 180% ATK. Larger targets still take 450% ATK.
  • Buff Green Heart of Empathy — ATK and MAG penalty -20% → -12%. No longer has a 30% chance not to gain “Mounting Rage”.
  • Buff Strongest There Is — now also grants +10% CRIT RATE and +10% CRIT.
  • Buff Desire for Solitude — HP restored 10% → 15%.
  • Rework Immeasurable Resistance — the 20% (30% below 35% HP) chance to nullify ULTIMATUMs is now a guaranteed nullify of the first ULTIMATUM received, once per battle, and it works on ULTIMATUMs that are guaranteed to land. The 500% ATK counter now requires the attacker to be within 2 panels.
  • Buff Immortal Hulk — now also grants “Taunt” for 2 turns and inflicts “Retribution” for 2 turns to all enemies within 4 panels.
  • Rework Synergies — Thunder Wave now mirrors Thunder Clap and adds “Shock” to all enemies hit (was 220% ATK single-target). Earth-Destroying Slam: outer shockwave 50% MAG within 8 panels → 200% MAG within 4 panels (it does not inflict “Bruise”). Overpowering Roar now keeps Confronting Roar’s effects and also restores 10% HP. Fastball Special now grants Wolverine “Pinpoint” on landing. Gamma Kamehameha now requires The Hulk to be within 2 panels of Goku. Green Bane of Asgardians now says the Loki must be an ally, not a Loki Clone.
  • Rework Links — removed “Tactical Mind”.
