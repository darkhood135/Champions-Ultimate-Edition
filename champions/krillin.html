Krillin

Krillin

Breaker

Goku's oldest and dearest friend, and the strongest ordinary human ever to walk the Earth, Krillin has spent a lifetime throwing himself into battles far beyond him — and never once backing down.

Krillin's whole life is a testament to the courage it takes to be outmatched and fight anyway. He began as a scheming little monk who came to train under Master Roshi alongside a strange boy named Goku, and what started as rivalry became the truest friendship either would ever know — the anchor around which his entire life would turn. As the world grew stranger and its threats grew into Saiyans, Namekians, androids, and gods, Krillin remained what he had always been: a human being, the very finest one the martial arts ever produced, and hopelessly outclassed by nearly everything he faced. That is exactly what makes him heroic. He has never had the power of his god-touched friends, and he has known it every time he stepped forward regardless — into fights he could not win, against monsters who could end him with a thought, because the people he loved needed someone to stand there. He has paid the ultimate price for that loyalty more than once, dying so that others might live and being called back by the Dragon Balls to do it all again. And in the end he did the wisest thing any of them ever managed: he set the fighting down, married a woman who was once his enemy, raised a daughter, and built a happy, ordinary life — finding the peace that all the warrior-gods around him chase and so rarely catch.

For all that he stands in the shadow of titans, Krillin is a warrior of genuine mastery. A disciple of the Turtle School, he commands strength, speed, and skill far beyond any ordinary person, and wields his ki with a precision the brawlers around him never bother to learn — loosing the Kamehameha, blinding foes with the Solar Flare, and above all spinning the Destructo Disc, a razor-thin blade of pure energy sharp enough to cut clean through enemies many times his better. Where raw power fails him, wit and technique carry him, turning cleverness into a weapon that has threatened beings who should have been leagues beyond his reach. But his power level was never the measure of Krillin. It was his heart — the ordinary man who kept standing shoulder to shoulder with legends, refused to run when running would have been only sensible, and earned, through sheer courage and loyalty, a place among the mightiest heroes the world has ever known.

"Even if I'm totally outclassed, I can't just sit here and do nothing!"

Catalyst of Awakening w/ Goku (Super Saiyan)

Krillin never wielded a fraction of Goku's power, and yet on Namek he changed the course of the universe all the same — not through any blow he struck, but through the life he lost. When Frieza murdered him before Goku's eyes, the grief and rage of it shattered something in his oldest friend and lit the golden fire of the first Super Saiyan in a thousand years. It is a bitter sort of legacy, that his death should be the spark for a legend. But it speaks to what Krillin always was to Goku: that of everyone in all the universe, it was the loss of him that drove the gentlest heart alive past its breaking point — because there was no one Goku loved, or would avenge, more fiercely.

Best Buddies of the Turtle School w/ Goku

Everything Krillin became began the day a strange, impossibly strong boy turned up to train under Master Roshi beside him. What started as rivalry between two young students grew into the deepest friendship of his life — decades of tournaments, adventures, and battles fought back to back. Goku is the reason Krillin kept getting stronger, the reason he keeps throwing himself into fights he has no business surviving, and the mark he's always cheerfully measured himself against. Through every death and every impossible stand, that bond has never once wavered: brothers in all but blood, forged in the same dojo and tempered by a lifetime side by side.

Mercy for Another w/ Vegeta

Krillin held the blade that could have ended Vegeta. Bloodied and beaten, the Saiyan prince lay helpless after the battle for Earth, and every reason in the world said to finish him — the monster had slaughtered their friends without a flicker of remorse. But Goku asked him to stay his hand, and Krillin, against his own better judgment, chose mercy and let the prince crawl away to live. He has never been entirely sure he did the right thing, and Vegeta has given him cause to doubt it many times since. Yet some quiet part of Krillin holds to it still — that sparing a life, even one like that, was worth the risk of whatever it might someday become.
