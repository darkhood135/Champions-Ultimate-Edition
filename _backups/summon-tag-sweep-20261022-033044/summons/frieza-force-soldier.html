Frieza Force Soldier

Loyal Follower of Frieza

Blaster

At a summons, one of the countless soldiers of the Frieza Force drops onto the battlefield — armored, scouter gleaming, and utterly expendable. Drawn from a hundred conquered worlds and welded into a single vast machine of empire, these troopers fight not out of loyalty but out of terror, for the only thing more dangerous than the enemy in front of them is the master who sent them. Individually they pose little threat to a true warrior; their blasts and blades were forged for purging the defenseless, not dueling the mighty. But they never arrive alone, and they never stop coming — because in Frieza's empire a soldier is not a person but a resource, endlessly replaceable, spent as freely as ammunition by a ruler who reckons their lives as worth precisely nothing.

"Lord Frieza!"
