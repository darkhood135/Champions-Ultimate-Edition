Frieza

Emperor Frieza

Summoner

The galactic emperor whose very name is dreaded across a thousand worlds, Frieza rules through terror and slaughter — a tyrant of near-limitless power hunting the Dragon Balls of Namek for the one prize his empire cannot seize: immortality.

Frieza is what absolute power becomes when it has never once been questioned. Emperor of an interstellar dominion built on conquest, he takes worlds, has them purged of every living thing, and sells the cleared husks to the highest bidder — an atrocity he conducts not in rage but with the bored elegance of a man tending to paperwork. That is what makes him so uniquely monstrous: he is soft-spoken, courteous, almost gentle in his manner even as he snuffs out civilizations, and he savors the fear and despair of others as a connoisseur savors fine wine. His arrogance is total and, until Namek, entirely earned — his power sits so far beyond any who might oppose him that he has never in his life been made to truly try. And beneath that serene supremacy lies the single fear that defines him: the legend of the Super Saiyan. It was that fear, and that alone, that drove him to annihilate the Saiyan race and their homeworld, murdering an entire people to smother a prophecy — then keeping the survivors as slaves to serve the very empire that orphaned them. The cruelest irony of his existence is that the genocide he committed to escape a legend is precisely what will one day call that legend into being.

The power he wields dwarfs anything the heroes of Namek have ever faced. His strength, speed, and durability tower over Saiyan and Namekian alike, and he commands his ki with casual, surgical precision — impaling foes on the thin crimson thread of his Death Beam, and gathering, at the tip of a single finger, the planet-shattering sphere with which he unmade the Saiyan homeworld in an instant. He bends others to his will with a thought, soars through the void of space where lesser beings would perish, and, most fearsome of all, carries so much raw power that he must bind it — locking his true might behind a succession of transformations, each shed like a mask to reveal something sleeker and more terrible, until at last stands his final form, purest and deadliest of them all. Yet the most dangerous thing about Frieza has never been his strength. It is that he has never had cause to fear losing it — and a tyrant who cannot imagine defeat is a tyrant who will never see it coming until it is upon him.

"None surpass me! No one even comes close! ... I am the Emperor of the universe! The likes of you are only fit to be rubble at my feet!"

The Legend He Feared w/ Goku (Super Saiyan)

For all his power, Frieza spent his life haunted by a single ghost story — the Super Saiyan, a warrior of such might that even an emperor might fall — and he exterminated an entire race to be certain it stayed a myth. On Namek, that myth looked him in the eye. In the low-class Saiyan he'd dismissed as vermin, risen gold and terrible over the corpse of the friend he'd just murdered, the tyrant met the one thing he never believed he would face: first an equal, and then his better. It is the great unraveling of his existence — living proof that the genocide he committed to escape a prophecy was the very thing that forged it, and that the universe he ruled without fear held, after all, something worth fearing.

The Tyrant's Leash w/ Vegeta

To Frieza, the Saiyan prince is a favored tool and nothing more — a sharp little knife he keeps precisely because it still fancies itself dangerous. He murdered Vegeta's father, burned his world to ash, and then kept the orphaned boy as a soldier of the very empire that did it, savoring the exquisite cruelty of a prince who kneels to the killer of his people. He knows full well that Vegeta hates him; he finds it delicious. And he holds not the faintest doubt that, should the day ever come when the little monkey's pride outgrows its usefulness, he will snuff that defiance out as casually as he has extinguished a thousand worlds.

Will Against Fear w/ Green Lantern

Frieza has conquered half a galaxy, and the notion that a single creature in a glowing ring would presume to stop him strikes the emperor as almost charming. To him the Green Lantern is a self-appointed lawman policing an empire that answers to no law but Frieza's own — an insolence to be corrected, not a threat to be feared. Yet there is something in the ring-bearer's refusal to yield that the tyrant has rarely met and does not care for: a will that simply will not break, worn by a being who should, by every measure of power, already have knelt. Frieza rules through fear — and here at last is one who refuses to be afraid, which is the one thing, above any weapon, that he cannot abide.

Changelog

Balance changes since this Champion's release. Newest first.

v1.0.1 September 21, 2026
  • Nerf "Precise" clarified system-wide: a Precise attack now lands as a guaranteed critical hit only against a blocking target (previously intended as an unconditional auto-crit). This affects Death Beam, Full Power Death Beam, and Full Power Death Ball, which now resolve as normal hits against non-blocking targets.
  • Buff Full Power Death Beam — primary hit increased from 600% to 630% MAG, and the secondary (pierce-through) hit from 300% to 400% MAG, so the transformed beam scales up rather than relying on the Full Power stat bump alone.
  • Rework Death Beam — corrected the secondary-hit wording to state that the 300% pierce-through damage is also piercing and precise, matching the primary hit.

The Precise clarification is the main change: narrowing the guaranteed crit to blocking targets keeps Death Beam a fair ~600% MAG snipe rather than an unconditional ~900%-effective one, while preserving its identity as a piercing, block-punishing tool. The Full Power Death Beam bump restores the intended power gap between the two forms now that the transformation no longer carries the crit, and the secondary-hit wording is a pure clarity fix.
