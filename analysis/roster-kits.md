# Roster Kits — condensed abilities (for deep peer comparison)

Companion to `roster-index.md` (stats). Read a champion's entry here when a critique needs ability-level comparison beyond stats. Format per ability: `Name — effect (key numbers)`. `[R]` = reserve. Transform forms shown as sub-sections.

**Maintenance / backfill:** Entries are added the first time a champion is reviewed or used as a comparison peer. When you finalize a champion in a critique, append/refresh its entry here in the same pass. Champions under "Pending backfill" aren't yet transcribed — read that champion's kit region (sheet offset ~985) once and add it here.

**Legend:** Type·EN·CD·Range where relevant. %ATK/%MAG = damage scaling. Numbers reflect the sheet at last review.
