Nagini

Cursed Witch

Striker

Long before she was the Dark Lord’s serpent, Nagini was a woman — a Maledictus, born with a blood curse that would one day take her shape for good. For years she could still shed her scales at will, but each change left less of her to come back to, until the last of her humanity slipped away and only the great snake remained: twelve feet of coiled muscle and venom, hidden in the forests of Albania. There a broken, bodiless Voldemort found her, and in her he found the one companion he would ever keep. Her venom fed the potion that kept his wretched half-life going until his rebirth, and later he sealed the final fragment of his soul inside her, making her a living Horcrux and the last anchor of his immortality. Bound to him in mind as well as magic, she answers only his hissed commands. She hid inside the hollowed shell of Bathilda Bagshot to lead Harry Potter into a trap at Godric’s Hollow, and she strikes from the dark with fangs whose cursed venom leaves wounds that refuse to close. She stays coiled at her master’s side, beyond the reach of any hand but his, the only creature the Dark Lord ever cared to protect. When Neville Longbottom finally drew the Sword of Gryffindor and cut her down, more than a serpent fell. The black radiance that had made Voldemort untouchable fell with her, and a cursed witch, after so long, was finally set free.

"This way."
