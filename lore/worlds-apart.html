Worlds Apart

Every universe as it was, before any of them knew the others existed.

Before the Incursion Crisis, every world on the roster ran on its own story, its own rules and its own heroes. Worlds Apart tells those stories one universe at a time, in arcs that follow each world’s canon (with the Archive’s own changes). Some characters’ stories end here, before the merge.
