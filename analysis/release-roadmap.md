# Champions Ultimate Edition — Release Roadmap

*Cumulative season-by-season plan for the in-fiction release timeline. Started 2026-10-29 · Lore wording updated 2026-11-02 · Sonic waves (Seasons 5–6) added 2026-11-15 · Year-one plan revised and year two planned 2026-11-15 (mixed seasons, Champions Fest).*
*Companion to `version-history-launch-plan.md`, which covers the launch itself and the patch-notes system. Lore terms follow `archive-architecture.md` (The Archive · Worlds Apart · Worlds Entwined).*

Each Season gets an entry here once it has a plan. Seasons with no plan yet stay in the calendar as open slots. Anything wanted but not yet scheduled goes in the Backlog at the bottom.

---

## Release caps

| Season type | Champions | Battlefields |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard season** | up to **5** | **1–2** |
| **Celebration season** | up to **10** | up to **3** |

- **Celebration seasons come in three kinds:**
  - **Anniversary Seasons:** the April 28 season every year, which runs from April into May (Season 13, Season 25, …). Fixed dates.
  - **Champions Fest:** the July 28 season every year **from 2028 on** (Season 16, Season 28, …), a mid-year celebration at the Anniversary's scale, built around especially hype Champions (in the spirit of Dokkan Battle's and Dragon Ball Legends' festivals). Fixed dates.
  - **Main Story Seasons:** any season that brings a significant Main Story release, such as an Act expansion (e.g. v2.0.0, Act II). No fixed date; they land whenever the story is ready.
- If a Main Story release ever falls on an Anniversary Season, it's still one celebration season with the same caps; the caps don't stack.
- Launch (Season 1) is its own scope (42 Champions, 7 battlefields) and isn't bound by these caps.

---

## Planning principles (2026-11-15)

- **Superheroes stay the centre of the roster.** Space out the big crossover franchises, with superhero or mixed seasons between them.
- **Mixed seasons are the default.** A mixed season spreads its Champions across franchises, e.g. a new Marvel and a new DC Champion, a Goku or Vegeta form or another Dragon Ball character, so every season has something for most players.
- **Arc-based seasons are low priority.** When one does happen, Dragon Ball goes arc to arc in story order (launch covers the Saiyan and Frieza sagas, so the Androids and Cell come next).
- **Every new franchise ships with a Leader inside its own links**, so it can be led as a team on arrival (the Team Sonic / Team Dark pattern).

## Season 7: Icons of Horror (v1.6.0, Oct 28, 2027)

**Theme:** Halloween. The horror icons arrive through a limited-time Halloween **Event Story**.

**New franchises:** Halloween, Friday the 13th, A Nightmare on Elm Street, Scream, The Texas Chain Saw Massacre.

### Champions (5 of 5)

| Champion | Real name | Role | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Shape | Michael Myers | Striker | Locked |
| Jason | Jason Voorhees | Defender | Locked |
| Freddy | Freddy Krueger | Breaker | Locked |
| Ghostface | Billy Loomis | Plunderer | Proposed |
| Leatherface | Bubba Sawyer | Berserker | Proposed |

- **Ghostface design note:** built as a duo with **Stu Macher**, a summon that spawns at the start of battle. Weak apart, strong together. Stu steals enemy buffs and pulls enemies in; Billy goes in for the execution.
  - The Stu sheet must say explicitly what happens to Stu if Billy is defeated, and vice versa. There's no roster-wide rule for summon persistence.

### Battlefields (2 of 2)
- **Lampkin Lane** (Haddonfield): Halloween.
- **Camp Crystal Lake:** Friday the 13th.
- Each gets 1–2 signature items, per the map-centered items rule.

### Also planned this Season
- **Halloween Event Story:** the in-fiction reason these characters enter the Cataclysm. Its Story Notes ship in v1.6.0.
- **The Archive:** a Worlds Apart universe portal for each new franchise, plus a character article for each new Champion.
- **Links:**
  - one Universe link per franchise;
  - a shared Link across all five (e.g. a "Slasher" Mindset link) with a Leader among them, so the horror team can actually be led under the Leader/Link economy.
- **Halloween costumes for existing Champions** (optional): a live-game staple, and it gives the Season something for players who don't use the new characters.

### Deferred from this Season
Laurie Strode, Ash Williams, Billy the Puppet, Sawyer Residence and Badham Preschool, now scheduled for Halloween #2 (Season 19).
